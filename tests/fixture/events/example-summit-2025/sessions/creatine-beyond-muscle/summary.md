# Creatine beyond muscle

## Summary
Dr Example argued that creatine's strength market is mature and that its next buyers are older adults who want it for memory and healthy ageing, not for lifting.

She built the case on a trial in older women and on the change she sees in who buys creatine. She was careful about the evidence: the cognition data may support a claim one day, she said, but it does not yet.
