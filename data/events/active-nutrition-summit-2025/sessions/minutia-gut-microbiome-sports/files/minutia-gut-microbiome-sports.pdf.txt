HOW IT WORKS
We collect a stool sample from the athlete
using our easy-to-use self-collection kit. We
also ask to complete a full anamnesis
questionnaire online. For best results, we
recommend collecting and analysing blood
and urine samples as well. Knowing baseline
physical parameters (e.g. VO2max) is also
required for maximum benefits and insights
The output is consolidated
into a comprehensive report
describing the findings and
providing an overall
assessment of your status,
along with targeted dietary
recommendations and
follow-up by our nutritionist
