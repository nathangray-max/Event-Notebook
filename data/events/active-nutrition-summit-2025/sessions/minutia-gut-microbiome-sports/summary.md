# Gut Microbiota & Sports

## Summary
Silvia Barbaresi, founder and chief scientific officer of Minutia.AI, presented as one of the summit's startup award winners, followed by questions from the moderator. Minutia uses probabilistic AI to predict how individuals respond to nutrition, starting with athletes.

Her argument: the gut microbiome is a network, so single biomarkers miss the picture. The gut connects to muscle, joints, brain and blood, and may shape performance and injury risk. Minutia tests stool samples with shotgun metagenomics, adds questionnaires and blood data, and returns performance and injury indices with a tailored diet. A digital twin of the athlete's gut is the goal for the next two to three years; she called the platform "under construction".

She showed early pilot results. Of 20 runners and triathletes, 12 completed: six on a personalised diet with prebiotics and probiotics for eight weeks, six controls. Some blood markers moved in the intervention group, but she said 12 people is not enough for big conclusions.

Minutia sells B2B to clinics and sports centres in several countries, is moving into longevity, and plans larger studies, transfer to the general population and partnerships with wearable makers. It does not work with brands yet but has not ruled it out.

This updates her February 2025 Probiota talk (probiota-2025 / minutia-gut-microbiota-sports).

## Key takeaways
- The thesis: the gut-muscle and gut-joint axes "play a key role" in performance and injury prevention, and interventions "are always customized" (slide 6) [06:33].
- The service: stool kit, shotgun metagenomics, anamnesis, ideally blood, urine and VO2max, then a report with performance and injury indices and dietary advice (slide 7) (slide 8) [06:33].
- The pilot: 20 recruited, 6 intervention and 6 control analysed, all male, VO2max 49 ± 7 ml/kg/min, age 46 ± 12, eight weeks of tailored diets with pre- and probiotics (slide 11) [06:33].
- Results: she said TIBC, total proteins, glucose and monocytes fell after the intervention [06:33]; the slide marks TIBC decreasing at adjusted p < 0.05 and shows no VO2max effect (slide 13). She said the sample is not enough "to make big assumption" [06:33].
- The digital twin is an ambition for "maybe two, three years"; the intelligent platform is under construction [06:33].
- Next: the largest gut microbiota collection in sport, transfer to the general population and longevity, and wearable partnerships (slide 16) [20:02].

## Detail
- Team: founder and CSO with a PhD in sports nutrition; an AI team with backgrounds in computer science, molecular biology, physics, microbiology, immunology and bioinformatics (slide 2). She said the company has nine people, began in Singapore five or six years ago with her co-founder, a data scientist, and that this is its first year in sport [03:52] [22:54].
- She mentioned a project in Singapore with Danone in the dairy field, as Otter heard it [03:52].
- Lab partner: cmbio for shotgun metagenomics (heard as "cm bio") [06:33].
- Correlations in the pilot (Spearman, Benjamini-Hochberg adjusted): Oscillospiraceae with creatinine 0.76 (p < 0.01); Bifidobacteriaceae with the Bifidobacterium shunt 0.89 (p < 0.001) (slide 12). She linked Oscillospiraceae and creatinine to high protein intake [06:33].
- Taxa: the slide marks Eggerthellaceae decreasing in two columns and Lachnospiraceae, Peptostreptococcaceae, Bifidobacteriaceae, Coriobacteriaceae, UBA1381 and Butyricicoccaceae decreasing in one; which column is which group is unclear in the PDF (slide 14). She said some Lachnospiraceae may matter for cardiovascular health and that species-level work is still to do [06:33].
- In the control group, she said, cholesterol increased [06:33]; the slide shows LDL and cholesterol increasing (slide 13).
- Clients: clinics and sports rehabilitation centres in Spain, Andorra, Italy and Australia; a US longevity clinic and one in Poland; performance athletes in soccer, tennis and motor sport, including Superbike champion Jules Cluzel (slide 10) [06:33].
- Choosing supplements: she picks strains from the literature, naming Lactobacillus plantarum for iron, and also uses cross-feeding between bacteria when a strain is not sold in a country [24:08] [26:21]. She recommends a retest after three months [20:02].
