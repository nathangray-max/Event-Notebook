Implications for nutrition and precision health
• We should evaluate the contributions of the microbiome based on
reflections in the host – especially the nutrients and toxins in the
metabolome
• Big need to more deeply map how diet and microbiome interact to
inform AI-guided personalization platforms
• Microbiome is a key component in healthy aging – and becomes
increasingly unique to each individual

--- page 21 ---
Conclusions
• AI represents a transformative opportunity
for personalization, allowing us to leverage
deep data
• Microbiome has profound effects on aging,
health, disease, and drug responses
• Genetics and dynamic measures can provide
a prioritization of where personalized
nutrition where most benefit likely to be seen
• AI insights powered by deep phenotyping
across health and disease will provide
revolutionary advances for AI-enabled 21st
century medicine
