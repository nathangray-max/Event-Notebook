Why is LBM Important?
• LBM is associated with healthy aging
• Mobility
• Avoiding falls
• More robust survivability
• LBM is associated with a higher metabolic rate
• BUT…Muscle is only one component of LBM

--- page 6 ---
Rapid Weight Loss, TDR & LBM?
• Broadly, weight loss reduces both body fat (BF) and lean
body mass (LBM)
• It does so in proportion to degree of weight loss
• Even usually when combined with exercise
• Bariatric surgery has a similar effect

--- page 7 ---
Exercise & LBM?
• When used for weight loss, exercise usually reduces LBM
• When combined with diet, Ex has small, often
insignificant additive effects on weight loss or LBM
• Sustained, supervised exercise results in modest weight
loss (~2kg) (Wing, 1999)
Wing RR. Physical activity in the treatment of the adulthood overweight and obesity:
current evidence and research issues. Med Sci Sports Exerc. 1999 Nov;31(11 Suppl):S547-52

--- page 8 ---
Exercise & LBM?
• Systematic review of studies on Ex, Ex + Diet & Diet alone (Weinheimer,
2010)
• Exercise & diet both resulted in >75% of subject groups losing from 0-
40% FFM
• Diet = loss of 5-10kg in 75% of groups
• Exercise resulted in < 5% loss in 94% of studies; 15% gained FFM
Weinheimer EM, et al. A systematic review of the separate and combined effects of energy restriction
and exercise on fat-free mass in middle-aged and older adults: implications for sarcopenic obesity. Nutr
Rev. 2010 Jul;68(7):375-88

Bariatric Surgery & LBM?
• Just over a year after surgery, 25-33% of losses were as LBM (Zalesin,
2010)
• Increasing proportion of LBM loss with > weight loss over one year
• Up to at least 35% LBM loss (Haghighat, 2021)
Zalesin KC, et al. Differential loss of fat and lean mass in the morbidly obese after bariatric surgery.
Metab Syndr Relat Disord. 2010 Feb;8(1):15-20.
Neda Haghighat, et al. Long-term effect of bariatric surgery on body composition in patients with
morbid obesity: A systematic review and meta-analysis,Clinical Nutrition, Volume 40, Issue 4, 2021, Pages
1755-1766.

Does rapid weight loss (food vs TDR) cause
disproportionately greater loss of LBM?
• In 101 Obese, PM Women: CR of ~30% x 12 mo. vs ~70% x 4 mo. (TDR)
• 2-fold greater weight and fat loss, approximately 1.5 times as much loss of
whole-body lean mass
• “Severe energy restriction had no greater adverse effect on relative whole-
body lean mass or handgrip strength compared with moderate energy
restriction and was associated with 2-fold greater weight and fat loss over 12
months”
Seimon R.V. Effect of weight loss via severe vs moderate energy restriction on lean mass and body composition among
postmenopausal women with obesity: the TEMPO diet randomized clinical trial. JAMA Netw Open. 2019;2(10)

Does rapid weight loss (TDR) cause
disproportionately greater loss of LBM?
• Female subjects Rapid (1.9 kg/week) vs Moderate (1.1 kg/week) over
8 weeks
• Proportion of weight loss as FFM
• Rapid = FFM/Wt loss of 0.42 vs Moderate = 0.44
Coxon A, et al. Rapid weight loss and lean tissue: evidence for comparable body composition and metabolic rate in differing
rates of weight loss. Int J Obes. 1989;13 Suppl 2:179-81.)

--- page 14 ---
Does rapid weight loss with TDR cause
disproportionately greater loss of LBM?
• 35 Obese Adults; 550 (TDR) x 4 wk vs 1200-1500 kcal x 8 wk
• Both had signiﬁcant & similar weight loss (~9%, P <0.001 for both
groups)
• Fat Mass & FFM losses were also similar
• RWL group lost weight at a rate of 2.2 kg/w Gradual = 1.2 kg/week
• In a 1-mo maintenance phase, RWL signiﬁcantly increased in FFM (P
<0.001)
Coutinho SR, et al. The impact of rate of weight loss on body composition and compensatory
mechanisms during weight reduction: A randomized control trial. Clin Nutr. 2018 Aug;37(4):1154-
1162.

Thoughts on lean body mass (LBM) & fat
free mass (FFM)
• People tend to think LBM (or FFM) loss is from muscle. ~ 40% of
LBM is muscle
• The rest is bone, skin, other organs, water & even some essential
fat components of cells, many of which are actually more
metabolically-active than muscle
• Research using DEXA, MRI & 4-C model show several other
organs shrink with weight loss (9.5kg) beyond just muscle
• Furthermore, it may be healthy for these other organs to shrink…
• Bosy-Westphal A, et al. Contribution of individual organ mass loss to weight loss-associated decline
in resting energy expenditure. Am J Clin Nutr. 2009 Oct;90(4):993

--- page 23 ---
Is some LBM Loss Desirable?
…Autophagy
• CR causes a process called “autophagy” – kind of like house cleaning
for cells or a 'cellular recycling processes’
• Research on fasting & calorie restriction show that this process can
help incr lifespan of everything from yeast & flies to primates
• the autophagy triggered by energy deficit leads to “a cytoplasmic
recycling process that counteracts the age-associated accumulation of
damaged organelles and proteins as it improves the metabolic fitness
of cells”
Madeo F, Zimmermann A, Maiuri MC, Kroemer G. Essential role for autophagy in life span extension. J Clin Invest.
2015;125(1):85-93.
Balasubramanian P . et al. Aging and Caloric Restriction Research: A Biological Perspective with Translational Potential.
EBioMedicine. 2017;21:37–44.
