# Sports Nutrition Innovation Insights

## Summary
A panel on the first afternoon, after the Euromonitor and Holland & Barrett talks: Nick Morgan, introduced as founder and managing director of Nutrition Integrated; Luca Bucchini, ESSNA's chair and a food supplement regulation expert; and Dr Mark Evans of Holland & Barrett. The chair is not named.

Morgan said protein and pre-workout remain the pillars of sports brands, but protein now runs through every format, and the further it spreads the simpler its message becomes. Protein waters were Morgan's model of simple innovation, and creatine Morgan's case study of an old ingredient reinvented through communication. Evans added that the science for premium protein forms over others is thin, and that sourcing quality ingredients is harder than Evans expected.

Bucchini covered regulation: claims rules differ by member state, and the Commission's coming maximum levels for vitamins and minerals look very low for several nutrients. Bucchini saw promise in botanicals but no convincing pro- or postbiotic sports products yet.

On white spaces, the panel named GLP-1 users, longevity and endurance, which Morgan said has never been unlocked. Their advice to founders: focus, believe in the product, and know the rules.

## Key takeaways
- Protein is proliferating into every format, and "all proteins are equal" in the mainstream; only powders keep the technical metrics of source, protein per serve and cost per serve [01:31] [04:33].
- Newer protein bars carry less protein: iconic bars stay at 20 g, newer launches sit at 10 to 15 g, Morgan said, possibly for price or accessibility [04:33].
- Evans: it is "very difficult to show any concrete data" that isolates or hydrolysed proteins beat other forms [02:45].
- Bucchini expects the Commission's maximum levels for vitamins and minerals to be very low, especially for magnesium, potassium, manganese, calcium, vitamin C and vitamin B6, and claims rules to vary by member state (Germany difficult, France liberal, Italy its own rules) [13:59] [15:48].
- White spaces: GLP-1 users moving into active nutrition, the shift from healthy ageing to longevity, and endurance beyond gels and summer hydration [30:23] [31:10] [32:27].
- Creatine is Morgan's case study of an ingredient reinvented into new areas through communication [08:57].
