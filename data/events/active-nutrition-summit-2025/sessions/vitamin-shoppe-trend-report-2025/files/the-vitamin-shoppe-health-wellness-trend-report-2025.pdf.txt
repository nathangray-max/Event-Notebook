Introduction
Driven by scientific innovation, highly engaged consumers, and a heightened focus on active lifestyles
and performance, the health and wellness sector is evolving in dynamic ways in 2025. The Vitamin
Shoppe’s fourth annual Health & Wellness Trend Report provides a data-rich analysis of this evolution,
powered by proprietary insights from our 635+ retail locations and vitaminshoppe.com, as well as
industry-wide data from SPINS.
T o further contextualize these insights, The Vitamin Shoppe commissioned an exclusive 2025 Consumer
Survey of 2,000 U.S. adults, conducted by leading market research firm T alker Research. *
This year’s report reveals a highly proactive wellness consumer, with 69% of Americans currently taking
dietary supplements to support their health and well-being. Motivations range from energy and stress
management to longevity, cognitive health, and weight management. Over half of Americans (56%) are
very or somewhat likely to try new trends to reach their wellness goals. Yet consumers are divided on
the overall trajectories of their personal health: 31% believe they are healthier than they were five years
ago, while 35% report feeling less healthy and 30% see no change, according to the survey.
“The insights from our Health & Wellness Trend Report 2025 underscore the challenges and complexities
of modern wellness, as well as the strong demand for the trusted products, tools, and expertise that
can help individuals optimize their unique wellness journeys, ” says Sharon Leite, CEO of The Vitamin
Shoppe.
Notably, AI is playing an increasingly vital role in that pursuit. Over one-third of Americans (35%) have
already used AI tools to manage or learn about their health, and another 27% would consider doing so in
the future. Among those who have used AI, popular applications include researching medical or wellness
topics (31%), meal and recipe planning (25%), weight-loss program design (24%), and learning about
supplement usage (21%).
Amid the myriad emergence of wellness trends online and in the media,
The Vitamin Shoppe’s Trend Report identifies five standout movements that
reflect a deeper convergence of scientific innovation, shifting consumer
priorities, and changes in lifestyle-driven wellness:
1. Protein Shifts to Functional Foods and Beverages
Once relegated to powders and gym routines, protein is fast expanding into anytime and anywhere
consumption through convenient bars, ready-to-drink (RTD) beverages, and snackable functional
foods.
2. The NAD+ Revolution for Longevity
Previously the domain of biohackers, NAD+ (nicotinamide adenine dinucleotide) has entered the
mainstream as a powerful supplement supporting cellular health, energy production, and anti-aging
goals.
3. Peptides: The Next Frontier in Sports Nutrition
Already well-known for their applications in the beauty and skin-care space, these potent, bioactive
compounds are emerging as a breakthrough innovation in sports nutrition for their performance-
enhancing and recovery benefits.
4. Delivery Formats Move Beyond Pills and Powders
Consumers are gravitating toward convenient and enjoyable supplement formats—such as liquids,
gummies, chews, beadlets, and topicals—that fit seamlessly into modern lifestyle routines.
5. A New Generation of Wellness Seekers Discovers Shilajit
Driven by social media, this time-honored adaptogen is experiencing renewed relevance among
modern consumers seeking its holistic wellness benefits.

Protein Demand Shifts to Functional
Foods and Beverages
Once dominated by powders, the protein category is being redefined as consumers increasingly look to
on-the-go, lifestyle-friendly formats that include protein-enhanced, ready-to-drink (RTD) beverages,

In 2025, The Vitamin Shoppe has seen year-to-date sales increases of 20% in RTD protein beverages
and 28% in protein bars and snacks, signaling a shift in consumer behavior toward convenience, taste,
and macro-balanced nutrition. Protein powders decreased by 12% in the same period.
“Bars and drinks are outpacing powders for the first time in years, with innovation, great taste, and
sharp macros driving momentum in these categories, ” says Jack Gayton, VP and General Merchandise
Manager at The Vitamin Shoppe. “Protein continues to dominate wellness routines, but RTDs have
emerged as a convenient alternative to the shaker cup, highlighting changing consumer patterns and a
growing demand for grab-and-go options. ”

protein supplement and meal replacement category in 2024, according to SPINS data. Searches for

macro.
In The Vitamin Shoppe Consumer Survey, 66% of respondents said they are actively trying to add more
protein to their diets.

Trend #2
The NAD+ Revolution for Longevity
Fueled by social media buzz, research breakthroughs, and brand marketing campaigns, NAD+
(nicotinamide adenine dinucleotide) has become one of the biggest longevity and cellular health trends
of the past year. Once the domain of biohackers and scientists, NAD+ is now gaining widespread
traction among health-conscious consumers seeking to optimize energy, slow the aging process, and
support metabolic function.
Case in point: Searches for NAD+ on vitaminshoppe.com skyrocketed nearly 500% this year. In total
sales, the cellular health and healthy aging category grew by 91% in Q1 at The Vitamin Shoppe, and
NAD+ products contributed 90% of that growth.
Why the immense interest? NAD+ is a coenzyme found in every living cell and is essential for numerous
biological functions, including energy production, DNA repair, and cellular resilience. It acts as a critical
molecule in metabolism because it helps to convert nutrients into cellular energy, which is fundamental
to all functions of the body. As we age, however, NAD+ levels decline—by up to 50% between 40 and 60
years old—which can lead to fatigue, reduced metabolic efficiency, cognitive decline, and other signs of
aging.

With NAD+ supplementation, users aim to counteract this decline by improving overall cellular
function, thus enhancing vitality and healthspan. These supplements can be NAD+ itself—which can be
challenging for the body to absorb due to the digestion process—or formulas that focus on precursor
nutrients that the body converts into NAD+, such as NR (nicotinamide riboside), NMN (nicotinamide
mononucleotide), and NAM (nicotinamide).
“There are many more human studies on precursors versus direct NAD+ supplementation, ” says
Brittany Michels, a Registered Dietitian Nutritionist at The Vitamin Shoppe. “The pro of taking NR, for
example, is that it’s well-absorbed and efficiently converted to NAD+ through established metabolic
pathways. The pro of taking NMN is that it’s one step away from becoming NAD+, which may result
in faster or more direct conversion. Making a choice between these supplements may come down to
specific health goals, formulation quality, preferences, and emerging research. While NAD+ precursors
currently offer the most reliable and accessible path to raising NAD+ levels, evolving formulations and
research may soon reshape the landscape. ”
Life Extension is the current NAD+ sales leader at The Vitamin Shoppe, posting 127% growth in Q1

NR.
Tru Niagen, developed by Niagen Bioscience—which is the patent holder for Niagen—is another top
brand, combining leading-edge science with robust marketing campaigns that have helped grow the
entire NAD+ category.
New market entrants like Codeage Liposomal NAD+ are addressing the absorption challenge with

Trend #3
Peptides: The Next Frontier in Sports
Nutrition
As science and consumer demand drive innovation in sports nutrition, a new class of bioactive
ingredients called peptides is poised to redefine the category. These powerful signaling molecules are
emerging as a breakthrough addition to traditional sports nutrition formulas because of the unique way
they support performance, recovery, and overall muscle health.
While peptides have already gained traction in beauty and wellness through the collagen boom, they are
now making significant inroads into performance nutrition. So, what exactly are peptides? In a nutshell,
they’re short chains of amino acids that act as biochemical messengers in the body, regulating processes
such as metabolism, appetite, blood sugar, inflammation, and muscle regeneration. Unlike amino acids
or proteins that serve as structural components, peptides influence how and when the body uses these
building blocks.
In the context of sports nutrition, peptides play a pivotal role in:
• Enhancing muscle protein synthesis
• Accelerating post-exercise recovery
• Improving endurance and strength
• Reducing fatigue and muscle soreness
Over the past year, The Vitamin Shoppe has been a leader in introducing peptide formulas within the
sports nutrition category—and customers have responded with robust interest. Searches for peptides
on vitaminshoppe.com have exploded over 550% in 2025.

Trend #4
Beyond Pills and Powders: The Rise of
Alternative Delivery Formats
While pill and powder formats have long dominated supplement sales, today’s wellness consumers are
actively seeking more convenient, enjoyable, and personalized ways to integrate supplements into their
routines. This growing demand has sparked a wave of innovation across the supplement industry, with
brands embracing new delivery systems that are both functional and user-friendly —from liquid vitamins
and gummies, to chews, beadlets, and even topical applications.
One of the most dynamic trends in supplement delivery is the surging popularity of liquid vitamins.
Sales in this category have jumped 50% year-to-date at The Vitamin Shoppe, led by category leader
MaryRuth’s, which has played a central role in mainstreaming liquid multivitamins in recent years.
MaryRuth’s has expanded its reach with innovative liquid offerings that feature additional targeted
health benefits. For instance, the top-selling Multivitamin + Hair Growth, plus an assortment of new
herbal tinctures targeting immune health. The brand’s impressive growth of 90% this year at The
Vitamin Shoppe underscores the widening appeal of liquid vitamins and supplements in expanding use
cases.
This momentum has inspired category stalwarts like New Chapter to enter the liquid space for the first
time. The launch of its Liquid Multivitamins in appealing berry and mango flavors marks a strategic
move to capture share in this growing format, supported by a robust national marketing campaign and a
strong retail presence.
Searches for “liquid multivitamin” on vitaminshoppe.com continue to grow, up 31% year-to-date in

Brands are also reimagining how consumers can experience functional ingredients in ever more creative

Trend #5
Shilajit: Modern T akes on an Ancient
Wellness Treatment
For centuries, shilajit has been used as a traditional Ayurvedic wellness treatment. Now, a new
generation of health seekers has rediscovered it, making it one of the past year’s trendiest—if also
stickiest—supplements. Shilajit sales were up over 40% in Q1 of this year at The Vitamin Shoppe, driven
by social media attention, a spate of new product launches, and renewed interest in its time-honored
wellness benefits.
Commonly sourced from mountainous regions like the Himalayas, this resin-like substance is formed
from the natural decomposition of plants and minerals. With its high concentration of fulvic acid—a
potent antioxidant that fights free radicals—and over 80 essential minerals like iron, magnesium,
potassium, and zinc, shilajit is a highly versatile supplement that can benefit a wide range of
demographics. Its users include older women seeking longevity and bone health benefits, men looking
for testosterone support, athletes aiming to enhance energy and muscle recovery, and people coping
with chronic stress or fatigue.
With these wide-ranging benefits, searches for shilajit have surged on vitaminshoppe.com, up 52% year
to date.
“Shilajit was once primarily known for supporting male vitality, but it’s now gaining broader appeal for
its potential benefits to energy levels, mental clarity, and hormonal health, ” says Brittany Michels, a
Registered Dietitian Nutritionist at The Vitamin Shoppe.
Launched in February at The Vitamin Shoppe, BetterAlt is redefining the category with modern
branding and innovative delivery systems. In just a few months, it has captured 25% of shilajit sales at
The Vitamin Shoppe, with product formats that include capsules, honey sticks, and gummies. The brand
has also combined shilajit with other trending ingredients, such as sea moss, ashwagandha, and tongkat
ali, offering synergistic benefits for testosterone, mood, and overall vitality. For purists, BetterAlt also
offers a resin product intended to be mixed into warm water or milk.

As shilajit has grown in visibility, brands are adding it as a hero ingredient to other core products, such
as JYM’s Alpha Advance T estosterone Optimization formula and Codeage’s GLP-Harmony Companion+

The multifaceted benefits of shilajit address a cross-section of the wellness concerns that consumers

The trends explored in this report point to a wellness space shaped by functional nutrition, advanced
ingredient science, and innovative delivery systems in 2025.
Emerging formats like protein-enriched functional foods and enhanced delivery technologies are
reshaping how consumers engage with supplements. Interest in cutting-edge ingredients such as
NAD+ and peptides underscores the mainstreaming of science-backed longevity and performance
optimization products.
While social media platforms like TikT ok are often credited for driving these types of trends, The
Vitamin Shoppe Consumer Survey revealed that traditional information sources still hold significant
sway. 15% of respondents cite TikT ok as a source of wellness discovery, but traditional touchpoints
remain on top: 30% learn about trends from friends, 29% from news media, 21% from ads, and 16%
from brands. The implication? Virality may spark curiosity, but long-trusted channels and word-of-
mouth still build genuine credibility and lasting awareness.
Indeed, trust remains a defining factor in trends, especially when considering new ingredients, products,
and formats. Consumers consistently place their highest levels of trust in doctors (59%), family (40%),
and friends (28%). On the other end of the spectrum, only 11% consider influencers “very trustworthy, ”
while 22% rate them “not trustworthy at all”—the lowest score across all sources, including AI and social
media.
For wellness consumers and brands alike, this divergence highlights the sustained importance of factors
such as clinical data and research, brand quality and transparency, and expert validation when it comes
to new trends and products. As this report illustrates, the future of wellness points towards categories
and products that fuse innovation with credibility and that can provide customers with trusted solutions
that suit their modern lifestyles.
