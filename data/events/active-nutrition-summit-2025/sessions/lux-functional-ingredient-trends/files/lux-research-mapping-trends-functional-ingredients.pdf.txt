Performance = increasing gains
over time
Performance = reducing the time
between exertions
Accelerating Recovery to Boost Muscle
Growth
The balance between exertion and
recuperation
There's a strong consumer focus on ingredients
that aid muscle recovery, seen as crucial for
maintaining and increasing muscle mass
through strength training.
After hitting the gym, I need something that helps my
muscles recover faster. It's not just about growth; it's about
sustaining what I gain.
Maximizing Muscle Mass and Strength
The quest for physical prowess and capability
Consumers seek functional ingredients that
promise enhanced muscle mass and strength,
aligning with their goals for resistance training
and muscle building.
I'm looking for something that'll pack on muscle and
strength. I want to see tangible results from my weight
training and high protein intake.
