Misuse of authority
• Accounts categorised as Docs had significantly
more followers on average (744,950) than
Hustlers (400,877) and Rebels (362,533)
• 59% had no formal health qualifications
• Only 7 out of 53 were verified medical doctors
• Only 1 internist among the 7 verified MDs
• 5 out of 53 held doctoral titles in non-medical
fields and prominently displayed “Dr”
Credit: Canva

The consequences
• In the European Region, 59% of adults and almost 1
in 3 children are overweight/obese1
• Promotes extreme, exclusionary diets
• SIMPLE SOLUTIONISM:
• Reduces nutrition to silver bullets (e.g. down to
animals vs. plants / calories in and out / cutting
carbs and sugar / fasting / UPFs are bad)
• Ignores the dangers of diet obsessions and the
value of dietary diversity
• ‘Tips’ contradict global guidance
• Fuels distrust in guidelines already under-followed
Credit: Canva
