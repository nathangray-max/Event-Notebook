# Personalised Health Panel

## Summary
A short panel on the second day, chaired by Olivia Haslam of NutraIngredients, with Mariette Abrahams (Qina), Marimiina Pykälistö (nutritionist and consulting advisor), Femke Hoevenaars (TNO) and a fourth panellist Otter does not name.

They agreed the field is moving from personalised nutrition to personalised health: lifestyle, mind and body, with tools that tell people what to do. Digital twins drew caution. Abrahams said fewer than five companies offer them and the term is used loosely; the others called them expensive, promising for research and not yet applicable.

On AI, Abrahams said it is spreading faster than the science and regulators can follow, and that advice built on unrepresentative data may not fit the individual. Weight remains the entry point, with GLP-1 users cycling on and off the drugs, but people link weight loss to energy, sleep and wider health, and a separate group wants longevity.

To build trust they called for transparency and plain explanations of why an ingredient helps. Asked for one change, Abrahams wanted proof of outcomes, Pykälistö statistics that show personalisation works, and the unnamed panellist a personal tool for health data.

## Key takeaways
- Personalised nutrition is becoming personalised health: "body of mind, soul nutrition" in Otter's words (Abrahams) and the whole lifestyle (Pykälistö) [00:42] [01:22].
- Digital twins: fewer than five companies offer them in personalised health or nutrition, by Qina's tracking, and many "digital twins" are just apps (Abrahams); expensive, promising in research, not yet applicable (Pykälistö, Hoevenaars) [03:02] [04:13] [04:40].
- AI is "proliferating" faster than the science and regulators; consumers love its simplicity but advice may not fit the individual (Abrahams) [06:02] [06:39].
- Weight management is still the gateway, with intermittent GLP-1 use, but people now connect weight to energy, sleep and menopause symptoms; longevity seekers are a separate group [08:43] [09:19].
- The missing piece is proof: "demonstrating outcomes, nutritional outcomes, behavioral" outcomes (Abrahams) [13:01].
