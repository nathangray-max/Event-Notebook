# NootroLabs: startup pitch (video)

## Summary
NootroLabs, one of the summit's two 2025 Start-Up Stars, is a UK contract manufacturer (Otter heard the name as "Neutral Labs"). Its co-founder and CEO, Cary Holmes (heard as "Harry"), could not attend and sent a short video, played before the Minutia.AI pitch.

Holmes set the company up in Staffordshire in 2021 and moved to a larger site in Worcestershire a year later, after winning retail contracts. The current site is 11,000 square feet, extended by another 3,000, and a second site of 10,000 square feet is being taken on, for 24,000 square feet across two sites by the end of the year.

It makes oils, capsules, gummies and powders. Best sellers are hemp oils, mushroom, magnesium and adaptogen capsules. Holmes said hemp is contracting for now and the adaptogen market is growing. The company is moving towards sports powders such as protein and pre-workouts, and aims to win large contracts, hence the new site. Holmes described it as debt free, with automated machinery and strong investors and mentors.
