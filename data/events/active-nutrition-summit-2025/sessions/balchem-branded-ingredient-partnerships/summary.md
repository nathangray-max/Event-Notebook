# Propelling a Stronger Future Through Partnership

## Summary
Jane Durga of Balchem, speaking in the female athlete session, presented Balchem's branded ingredients for active women and its sports partnerships.

She opened with partnership: Balchem sponsors the FC Bayern Munich women's football team, paired on her slide with its vitamin K2 brand, K2VITAL, to raise awareness of women's health and create role models. She then walked through the range "on the field": K2VITAL for bones, heart and muscle; VitaCholine, choline for the brain-muscle connection; and Creatine MagnaPower, a creatine-magnesium blend that she said improves performance where creatine monohydrate plus magnesium did not. "Off the field" came OptiMSM for recovery.

Her nutrient-gap argument ran through iron, choline and folate. Athletes lose more iron than other people and may need up to 70% more; only 7% of US adult women get enough choline from food, and falling oestrogen at menopause limits the body's own choline supply. Chelated iron and methylfolate, she showed, are absorbed better than the common forms.

Her conclusion: women are helping shape and expand sports nutrition, and well-absorbed minerals, choline, K2, folate and MSM serve them through every life stage.

## Key takeaways
- Only 7% of adult women in the US get enough choline from food and beverage (USDA, NHANES 2017 to March 2020) (slide 5).
- Choline in one study: targeting 9% closer and 2.6 times fewer misses in a visuomotor task against placebo (Naber et al. 2015) (slide 5).
- Oestrogen helps the body make choline and declines at menopause, so "For Menopausal Athletes, Ensuring Adequate Choline Intake is Important" (slide 6).
- The Institute of Medicine suggests the iron requirement "is up to 70% greater for those who engage in regular exercise" (slide 9).
- Absorption: Ferrochel iron bisglycinate 4.7 times higher than ferrous sulphate; L-5-MTHF 2.25 times higher plasma levels than folic acid (slide 10).
- Creatine MagnaPower, in one study, improved a performance measure that creatine monohydrate plus magnesium oxide did not (Brilla et al. 2003) (slide 7).

## Detail
- Slide numbers are the PDF's pages (12). The title slide names her and "Women’s Health" (slide 1). The closing slide says the information is for industry professionals, not consumers, that explicit or implied claims in it "may not necessarily be appropriate for marketing purposes", and is copyright 2024 Balchem (slide 12).
- K2VITAL, with the slide's hedges: MK-7 "may help trained athletes maintain maximal cardiac output during aerobic exercise"; K2 "supports ATP production"; K2 MK-7 "supports calcium uptake for strong bones and plays a role in the maintenance of bone mineral density" (slide 4). Slide 2 pairs K2VITAL with FC Bayern Munich Women as "The Perfect Pair" (slide 2).
- Choline is a component of cell membranes and of the neurotransmitter acetylcholine, "critical for the connection between your brain and muscles"; it is found mainly in animal foods such as eggs and beef, and the body makes some but not enough (slide 5) (slide 6).
- Creatine MagnaPower: "a unique blend of creatine and magnesium" with "no loading phase required"; claims of improved cellular swelling and anabolic signalling, muscle power and torque, sprinting and bench press work capacity. The chart compares placebo, MgO plus creatine and Creatine MagnaPower before and after, with only Creatine MagnaPower significantly different from baseline; its y-axis is not labelled (slide 7).
- OptiMSM is "a source of sulfur", a component of glutathione; MSM supplementation "has been shown to" reduce oxidative stress after exercise, post-exercise pain and muscle damage, and improve the antioxidant response. The charts show lower pain change at 2 hours and lower exercise-related pain after leg extensions with OptiMSM 3,000 mg a day for 14 weeks (N=24, mean age 25.5) (slide 8).
- Athletes experience greater daily iron loss than the general population, especially in endurance sports such as running, rowing, HIIT, cycling and basketball; younger athletes need iron too (slide 9).
- Her five closing takeaways also include chelated minerals for "a time of lower micronutrient intake", choline for "cognition and active aging", and K2 and folate in "high quality, pure, stable forms"; Balchem names the NY Jets and FC Bayern Munich as team partners (slide 11).
