Ketogenic diet – unexpected consequences
• 53 healthy adults for up to 12 weeks
Followed a
• moderate sugar diet (control)
• a low-sugar diet (less than 5% of calories from sugar)
• a ketogenic (keto) low-carbohydrate diet (less than 8% of
calories from carbohydrates).
• Both sugar restriction and keto diets achieved fat loss without
changing physical activity levels. Keto Diet resulted in an
average of 2.9 kg fat mass loss per person, whilst the sugar
restricted diet followed with an average 2.1 kg fat mass loss
per person at 12 weeks.
• The keto diet raised cholesterol levels, particularly in LDL. In
contrast, the low-sugar diet significantly reduced cholesterol in
LDL particles.
• Glucose Tolerance: The keto diet reduced glucose tolerance,
meaning the adults’ bodies became less efficient at handling
carbohydrates.

Ketogenic diet – unexpected consequences
• The keto diet altered gut microbiome composition, notably decreasing bifidobacteria.
• Sugar restriction did not significantly impact the gut microbiome composition.
