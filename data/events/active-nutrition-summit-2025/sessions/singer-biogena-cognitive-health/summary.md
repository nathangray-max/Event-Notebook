# Cognitive Health

## Summary
Kristiina Singer, a clinical nutritionist who has worked for Biogena for over ten years, opened the summit's last day with the case for natural cognitive support. Biogena is an Austrian family company that makes its own supplements near Salzburg and runs a flagship store in Vienna.

Their argument started with demand. Cognitive health is no longer only a concern of older people: in US data Singer showed, memory loss is the health problem adults under 40 fear most, and in Germany dementia is feared second only to cancer. Dementia cases are expected to reach 75 million worldwide by 2030 and roughly double by 2050.

Singer then turned to diet. The MIND diet, built on the Mediterranean and DASH diets and rich in berries and leafy greens, was linked in one study to a cognitive age 7.5 years younger. Polyphenols, and anthocyanins in particular, may help through new neurons, synaptic plasticity and protection against oxidative stress. Citicoline, a precursor of acetylcholine that crosses the blood-brain barrier, showed memory and attention effects in old and young people.

Biogena combines both, with activated B vitamins, in one product. Its sales are growing; over 60% of buyers are over 45, and about 16% are aged 25 to 35.

## Key takeaways
- Cognitive health is a concern for younger adults too: in US data from 2022, memory loss is the health problem people under 40 fear most [00:50].
- A high MIND diet score was associated in one study with a cognitive age 7.5 years younger; Singer framed polyphenols as central to the diet [00:50].
- Polyphenol studies Singer showed: 12 weeks of a blueberry powder standardised to 302 mg anthocyanins in 61 people over 65 improved flow-mediated dilation, systolic blood pressure and immediate recall; a single 600 mg dose of a polyphenol-rich extract in 30 students aged 18 to 25 gave more correct answers and fewer errors than placebo [00:50].
- Citicoline studies Singer showed: brain ATP up 13.6% and membrane turnover up 26% in one study; better attention and psychomotor speed and less impulsivity in 75 healthy males aged 13 to 18; better overall and episodic memory in older adults [00:50].
- Biogena's citicoline, polyphenol and activated-B-vitamin product is its fastest-growing: over 60% of buyers are over 45, and about 16% are 25 to 35 [00:50].

## Detail
- Biogena, as Singer described it: an Austrian family business for 20 years, production near Salzburg, over 300 products, a scientific team of 20, every batch checked by a third-party laboratory in Germany, and a focus on avoiding technical additives and raising bioavailability. The Vienna flagship store near the State Opera also offers diagnostics with doctors, laboratory tests, infusions and a biohacking area (cryotherapy, bone density checks, altitude training, light therapy) [00:50].
- An audience poll on what Germans fear most: the room put Alzheimer's and dementia first; the data Singer showed puts cancer first and Alzheimer's and dementia second, ahead of stroke and heart attack [00:50].
- Dementia cases worldwide: 75 million expected by 2030, with newer data saying over 82 million; 135 million by 2050, with a newer, higher figure Otter did not catch clearly [00:50].
- A review found the Mediterranean, DASH and MIND diets can reduce the risk of dementia and Alzheimer's disease; a 2024 Nature Medicine article said the MIND diet modifies risk factors such as hypertension, obesity and type 2 diabetes, which "could delay" dementia [00:50].
- Long-term berry and plant intake over 10 to 13 years has been associated with slower cognitive decline [00:50].
- The student study measured raised plasma flavonoids after the extract; Singer called it a safe way to acutely improve working memory and attention in healthy young people [00:50].
- Citicoline has been used as a neuroprotective drug since the 1970s and 1980s; Singer said it has good bioavailability and stabilises neuron membranes [00:50].
- Q&A: on dietary silica and aluminium, Singer said studies show an impact of aluminium and mercury but had no data to hand [28:19]. On mood, Singer treats it separately; Biogena has other products for mood and stress, one with 5-HTP [29:16]. The MIND diet limits fruit relative to vegetables partly because of sugar and fructose [29:16].
- The overview report attributes the 60% and 16% buyer figures both to this talk and to the Avea longevity talk (summit-overview-report, chabloz-avea-longevity-supplements).
