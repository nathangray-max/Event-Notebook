# Consumer health survey: 9 countries, 6,000 people

## Summary
An unnamed presenter shared results from a consumer survey run in November in nine countries with 6,000 people, covering health goals, health tech, biotics and sports nutrition. Otter heard the team as "our women and Intelligence Team"; the survey matches Lumina Intelligence's, but the company is to confirm. The presenter said they were not the data person and pointed to a colleague, Ed, for questions.

Getting better sleep was the top health goal overall. Sleep and stress lead for younger people; healthy ageing rises with age. Younger consumers track themselves: the presenter said 78 to 79% use a fitness tracker, and 91% of app users buy an app-recommended product at least once a year, mostly in physical stores.

Six percent of respondents said they regularly take GLP-1 drugs, more than the presenter expected outside the US.

On sports nutrition, 36% in Europe take none, against under 20% in the US. Protein leads everywhere. Consumers buy for muscle, but the room's guess that consumers understand creatine's benefits was wrong: they did not know, which the presenter called an opportunity. Price is the second reason for not buying in every country.

Their close: sports nutrition is broadening, consumers still think it is about muscle, and women are an underserved group who want more products.

## Key takeaways
- Better sleep was the top health goal overall; sleep and stress lead for younger people, healthy ageing and longevity rise with age [02:49].
- 91% of app users buy app-recommended products at least once a year: 22% monthly, 48% quarterly, 19% once a year; most buy in physical stores [02:49].
- 6% of respondents regularly take a GLP-1 drug [02:49].
- 36% in Europe take no sports nutrition supplement, against less than 20% in the US; protein leads in both [02:49] [07:57].
- Consumers did not know what creatine does, which the presenter called an opportunity; 76% of creatine users surveyed were men, and women named endurance as a reason [10:41] [11:15].
- Price is the number two reason for not taking sports nutrition in every country surveyed [11:15].

## Detail
- No deck was filed; every figure is as Otter heard it.
- Survey: run last November, nine countries, 6,000 people, with questions on general health and wellness, sports nutrition, biotics and the microbiome, and tech [00:31].
- A room poll guessed healthy ageing as the top goal (heard as 33%) [02:42]; the survey's answer was better sleep [02:49].
- Trackers: fitness trackers are the most used type, at 56% [02:49]. Strava is among the top four fitness apps [02:49].
- GLP-1: the presenter cited an independent source for 6% of the US population on a GLP-1 drug, "about 50 million Americans" [02:49]. GNC was the first US retailer with an in-store GLP-1 support display, covering gastrointestinal effects, nutrient deficiency, skin and muscle loss; one US retailer also offers the medications [02:49].
- Sports nutrition in the US: electrolyte hydration ranks fourth, ahead of creatine at 6%, a level similar to Europe [07:57]. In Europe creatine and hydration follow protein; the exact ranks are unclear in the recording [02:49].
- Reasons for use: muscle repair, growth, strength and soreness, then energy and endurance; younger people focus on muscle, older people on energy [07:57].
- Satisfaction: in the US 40% very and 31% somewhat satisfied, 71% in all; in Spain 33% somewhat and 13% very satisfied [07:57].
- Reasons for not using: in the US the top reason is eating a good diet (as heard); top reasons differ by country, with price second everywhere [11:15].
- Older people move less, so they need more resistance training [11:15].
- Another view of the same survey: Lumina Intelligence's Probiota 2025 deck (probiota-2025 / lumina-biotics-unleashed) gives 93% of app users buying an app-recommended product at least once a year, against 91% said here, and ranks ageing in good health first or second in five European countries, with better sleep first in France and the UK. The two figures differ; both are given.
