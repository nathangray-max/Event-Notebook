**Two Waves** is a digital platform designed to streamline the manufacturing ecosystem between brands, manufacturers, and ingredient suppliers.

## Market Challenges
The manufacturing market is projected by **Grandview Research** to grow at over 12 percent annually, exceeding $60 billion by 2030. However, current processes suffer from significant inefficiencies:
* Lack of visibility in specific product categories.
* Incomplete inquiries and fragmented communication across multiple email threads.
* High operational costs due to wasted time organizing data and searching for agreements.
* Lost opportunities resulting from missed offers or inquiries that do not reach the right partners.

## Platform Solutions
**Viktor** and **Matthew** introduced **Two Waves** as a tool to centralize quoting and negotiation through several key modules:
* **New Partnerships**: Instead of one-to-one outreach, one inquiry automatically reaches all verified manufacturers within a specific category, allowing for rapid comparison of structured offers.
* **Standardized Quoting**: The platform uses structured templates tailored to product categories to eliminate "communication noise" and ensure all key information is present.
* **Negotiation Management**: Users can manage quoting variants and track the full history of negotiations in a single location. 
* **Feedback Loops**: If an inquiry or offer is declined, the system provides a specific reason to improve future interactions.

## Security Infrastructure
**Matthew** addressed the risks associated with traditional communication methods like phone calls and emails, where data control is easily lost once information enters an inbox. To mitigate these risks, **Two Waves** implements:
* Mandatory company verification for all users to ensure participants are interacting only with verified entities.
* Enhanced control over how sensitive information is shared during the initial stages of business relationships.
