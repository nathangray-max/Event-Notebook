# 2waves: one platform for brands, manufacturers and ingredient suppliers

## Summary
Viktor Tsevsky and Matthew Kwembowski, co-founders of 2waves, pitched a platform for the three sides of the supplement market: brands, manufacturers and ingredient suppliers.

They started from a growing market, which Grand View Research puts at more than 60 billion dollars by 2030, and from how badly its sides find each other. Brands have no view of their category, offers come in no common format, inquiries are incomplete or do not fit, one inquiry runs across many email threads, and many get no answer at all. That costs time and lost business.

Their answer is a first module for new partnerships, quoting and negotiation. One inquiry reaches every manufacturer or supplier in the category. Offers come back in one structure to compare, the negotiation history stays in one place, inquiries are matched to who can make them, and every decline carries a reason. Matthew had begun on data security, with every company verified before joining, when the recording ended.

The conference organiser, Pawel Gruszczynski, sits on the project's board as strategic advisor.

## Key takeaways
- The market is framed as three sides, brands, manufacturers and ingredient suppliers, with Grand View Research's forecast of over 12 percent growth a year to more than 60 billion dollars by 2030 [00:13].
- The cost of today's process is operational (hours organising inquiries, hunting agreements in email threads) and lost opportunity (offers never received, inquiries never answered) [01:50].
- One inquiry reaches all manufacturers (or suppliers) in the category; offers come back in one structure, "two offers or 15", ready to compare [02:46].
- For suppliers and manufacturers: inquiries are matched to what you can produce, so you see opportunities that today never find you [02:46].
- Every company is verified before joining; the security module answers losing control of information once it sits in an inbox [07:42].
- Pawel Gruszczynski (Remedy, the conference organiser) is strategic advisor and board member of the project [02:46].
