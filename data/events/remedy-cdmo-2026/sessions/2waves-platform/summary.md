# 2waves: one platform for brands, manufacturers and ingredient suppliers

## Summary
Viktor Tsevsky and Matthew Kwembowski, co-founders of 2waves, pitched a platform for the three sides of the supplement market: brands, manufacturers and ingredient suppliers [02:46]. The market case was Grand View Research's forecast of over 12 percent growth a year and more than 60 billion dollars by 2030, set against the problems they listed: no visibility in your category, no single format for offers, incomplete inquiries, one inquiry across many email threads, inquiries that do not fit, and no answer at all [00:13]. These cost operating time and lost opportunity [01:50]. Module one, "new partnerships, quoting, and negotiation", sends one inquiry to every manufacturer or supplier in the category, returns offers in one structure to compare, keeps quoting variants and negotiation history in one place, matches inquiries to who can make them, and gives a reason for every decline [02:46]. Matthew began on data security, every company verified before joining, when the recording ended [07:42]. Pawel Gruszczynski, the conference organiser, was named as the project's strategic advisor and board member [02:46].

## Key takeaways
- The market is framed as three sides, brands, manufacturers and ingredient suppliers, with Grand View Research's forecast of over 12 percent growth a year to more than 60 billion dollars by 2030 [00:13].
- The problems named: no visibility in your category, no single offer format, incomplete inquiries, one inquiry in many threads, inquiries that do not fit, no answer at all [00:13].
- The cost is operational (hours organising inquiries, hunting agreements in email threads) and lost opportunity (offers never received, inquiries never answered) [01:50].
- One inquiry reaches all manufacturers (or suppliers) in the category; offers come back in one structure, "two offers or 15", ready to compare [02:46].
- For suppliers and manufacturers: inquiries are matched to what you can produce, so you see opportunities that today never find you [02:46].
- Structured templates per product category, negotiation history one click away, and a reason for every declined inquiry or offer [02:46].
- Every company is verified before joining; the security module answers losing control of information once it sits in an inbox [07:42].
- Pawel Gruszczynski (Remedy, the conference organiser) is strategic advisor and board member of the project [02:46].
- Pricing, launch date and current users were not given before the recording ended (my reading).
