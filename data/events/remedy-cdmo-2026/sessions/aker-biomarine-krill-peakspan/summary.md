# From Longevity to Peakspan: The Role of Krill Oil in Healthy Aging

## Summary
Aker BioMarine pitched its Superba Krill oil to Remedy's contract manufacturers as an ingredient for healthy ageing. Its frame is "Peakspan": not how long we live, or how long we avoid disease, but how long we stay close to our personal best. Consumers put it in plainer words: maintaining mobility, keeping physically active and keeping an active mind.

The deck opens on the market. Europe's longevity supplement market is put at $0.50 billion in 2025, rising to $1.23 billion by 2035. It then names seven areas that come under pressure with age, from muscle and joints to heart and skin, and gives trial evidence for krill oil in five of them.

The muscle evidence leads: in 102 adults over 65 taking 4 g a day for six months, strength rose against placebo. A weight-loss trial showed more of the weight lost coming from fat and less from muscle. Knee pain, triglycerides and skin follow.

It closes with three product concepts built on two of its ingredients, labelled as positioning directions, not approved claims, and a projection that fish oil supply falls short of demand from 2027.

## Key takeaways
- The frame: lifespan is how long we live, healthspan how long we avoid disease, "Peakspan" how long we stay close to our personal best; consumers rank mobility, staying active and an active mind as the top three aspects of healthy ageing (Innova survey, 11 countries) (slide 4).
- Muscle: in a six-month randomised, double-blind trial of 102 adults aged 65+ on 4 g a day (University of Glasgow), knee extension strength rose 9%, grip strength 11% and muscle thickness 3.5% against placebo (slide 7).
- Weight loss: in a 12-week trial (52 randomised, 41 completed, BMI over 25, alternate-day fasting), both groups lost about 4.5 kg, but 96% of the weight lost was fat on krill oil against 73% on placebo; fat-free mass fell 0.2 kg against 1.2 kg (slide 9).
- Joints: knee pain fell 18% against 13% on placebo (p=0.04) in 235 adults aged 40 to 65 with mild-to-moderate knee osteoarthritis; the deck cites a TGA assessed-listed knee claim in Australia (slide 8).
- Supply: Aker's own projection has fish oil demand overtaking supply from about 2027, with a gap of 0.16 million tonnes by 2031 and aquaculture taking most of the demand (slide 13).
- The three concepts (Peak, Renew, Strength) run on two products, Superba2 and Superba Boost, and are "positioning directions, not approved on-pack claims" (slide 12).

## Detail
- Market (Future Market Insights 2025): EU longevity supplements $0.50 billion in 2025 to $1.23 billion in 2035, 9.4% a year, 146% in total; largest markets Germany, France, the UK and the Netherlands. The slide warns this definition is narrower than NBJ's US "Healthy Aging" category (slide 3).
- Other figures on the market slide: 60% of consumers in the UK, Germany, the US and China rate healthy ageing a top or very important priority (McKinsey 2025, n over 9,000); 22% of the world aged 60 or over by 2050 (WHO); "cellular health" claims up 135% across 32 countries in the first half of 2024 (Euromonitor) (slide 3).
- The seven areas: cellular health, muscle, joints, heart, metabolism, skin, and brain and eyes. Muscle: 3 to 5% of muscle mass is lost every decade from the 40s, and strength declines faster than mass; osteoarthritis and chronic pain are the main reasons adults over 55 stop being active (slide 5).
- Muscle trial: Alkhedhairi et al., Clinical Nutrition 2022. The grip gain is described as comparable to the difference across a 10-year age gap (65 against 55) (slide 7).
- Joints: Stonehouse et al., American Journal of Clinical Nutrition 2022; in the high-CRP subgroup knee pain fell 27% against 7% (p<0.001). A 12-week pilot in 40 adults over 60 with hip, knee or lower-back pain (Superba Boost, 4 g a day) showed improvement in 67% against 40%; the slide calls it a feasibility study, not powered for efficacy (Tamargo et al., 2026) (slide 8).
- Weight loss: Alblaji et al., Obesity 2025. Grip strength fell 0.2 kg against 0.9 kg, chair-rise performance improved against no change, and the omega-3 index rose from 5.5% to 8.2% against 5.5% to 5.3%; blood pressure, TNF-alpha and CRP also improved against placebo (slide 9).
- Heart: in 520 adults with severe hypertriglyceridaemia at 164 centres, 4 g a day for 26 weeks lowered triglycerides 33.5% against 20.8% on placebo, and 37.4% against 16.1% in those already on statins, without raising LDL (Mozaffarian et al., JAMA Network Open 2022); two meta-analyses cited (slide 10).
- Skin: Superba Boost at 1 g and 2 g improved hydration, barrier function and elasticity against placebo, dose-dependently (Handeland and Wakeman, Journal of Cosmetic Dermatology 2024) (slide 11).
- Fish oil model assumptions: aquaculture demand and supplement demand each growing 3% a year, pet food 4%, and a 5% shortfall in each El Niño dip (2023, 2026, 2029). The slide is marked "Confidential" by Aker (slide 13).
