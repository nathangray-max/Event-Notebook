# Nuritas: AI-discovered plant peptides and the PeptiStrong trials

## Summary
Miguel (business development) and Brian Keogh (chief scientific officer) of Nuritas, Dublin, presented the company's own AI platform for finding bioactive peptides and the evidence for PeptiStrong, a fava bean protein hydrolysate. The AI takes a mechanism of action or a consumer need and returns candidate peptides for in vitro and in vivo work, about two years from first hypothesis to result [00:20]. PeptiStrong is said to target the mTOR pathway to build muscle and the NF kappa B pathway to lower inflammation; in a murine hind-limb unloading model it prevented 50% of the muscle loss [03:56]. Three human trials were shown: an immobilisation study in the Netherlands against a milk protein concentrate control, where strength came back to baseline and muscle protein synthesis was higher; a 14-day study in a Dublin sports clinic where strength went above baseline within two and a half weeks of a damaging exercise bout; and a KGK trial in Canada in untrained men and women, with faster strength gains at day 28 and 56, lower GDF15 and more bone mineral content [03:56]. Nuritas says PeptiStrong boosts protein rather than competing with it, and could add to creatine, leucine and HMB [03:56]. Claims rest on EFSA protein claims; Vivatis is the partner in Central and Eastern Europe, Nuritas has been active in Poland for six months, and it is developing a product with Tantus [13:50].

## Key takeaways
- Nuritas's proprietary AI goes from a mechanism or consumer need to peptide candidates, then in vitro and in vivo, in about two years on average [00:20].
- PeptiStrong is a protein hydrolysate from fava bean; the speaker says its peptides target mTOR (muscle building) and NF kappa B (inflammation) [03:56].
- Keeping muscles healthy was linked to a 17% decrease in all-cause mortality [03:56].
- Murine hind-limb unloading: PeptiStrong prevented 50% of the muscle loss [03:56].
- Immobilisation trial (Netherlands, males only): the control was milk protein concentrate, not a placebo; muscle lost and regained was the same, but strength came all the way back to baseline and FSR was higher with PeptiStrong [03:56].
- Damage trial (Dublin sports clinic, 14 days' supplementation, isokinetic dynamometer): strength above baseline within two and a half weeks; irisin rose then returned to normal, myostatin was tracked [03:56].
- KGK trial (Canada, untrained men and women, DEXA, supervised training): stronger quicker at day 28, kept at day 56; GDF15 fell with training load; bone mineral content rose, "a kind of a serendipitous finding" [03:56].
- Nuritas positions PeptiStrong as a booster of protein and a partner for creatine, leucine and HMB: "we're not competing with protein" [03:56].
- Claims on pack rest on EFSA protein claims; the regulatory manager has been "creatively defining" others [13:50].
- A library of more than 9 million peptides; next is a non-hormonal sleep peptide, formulated by Vivatis for CEE; six months active in Poland; a development project with Tantus [13:50].
