# Nuritas: AI-discovered plant peptides and the PeptiStrong trials

## Summary
Miguel, business developer, and Brian Keogh, chief scientific officer, presented Nuritas, a Dublin R&D company that uses its own AI platform to find bioactive peptides. The AI starts from a mechanism of action or a consumer need and returns candidate peptides for lab and animal testing, in about two years on average.

Keogh then made the case for PeptiStrong, a fava bean protein hydrolysate. The speaker says its peptides switch on muscle building through mTOR and calm inflammation through NF kappa B. In mice it prevented half the muscle lost when the hind legs were unloaded. Three human trials followed. After immobilisation, strength came back to baseline against a milk protein control. After a damaging exercise bout, strength rose above baseline within two and a half weeks. In untrained men and women, strength rose faster, with more bone mineral content as an unexpected finding.

His conclusion was that PeptiStrong boosts protein rather than competing with it, and may add to creatine, leucine and HMB.

Miguel closed on the commercial side: on-pack claims rest on EFSA protein claims, a non-hormonal sleep peptide is next, Vivatis is the partner in Central and Eastern Europe, and Nuritas is developing a product with Tantus.

## Key takeaways
- PeptiStrong is a fava bean protein hydrolysate; the speaker says its peptides target mTOR (muscle building) and NF kappa B (inflammation) [03:56].
- Immobilisation trial (Netherlands, males only): the control was milk protein concentrate, not a placebo; muscle lost and regained was the same, but strength came all the way back to baseline and FSR was higher with PeptiStrong [03:56].
- KGK trial (Canada, untrained men and women): stronger quicker at day 28, kept at day 56; GDF15 fell with training load; bone mineral content rose, "a kind of a serendipitous finding" [03:56].
- Nuritas positions PeptiStrong as a booster of protein and a partner for creatine, leucine and HMB: "we're not competing with protein" [03:56].
- On-pack claims rest on EFSA protein claims; the regulatory manager has been "creatively defining" others [13:50].
- Commercial map: Vivatis formulates for Central and Eastern Europe, Nuritas has been active in Poland for six months, and it is developing a product with Tantus [13:50].

## Detail
- Nuritas is an R&D company in Dublin; its proprietary AI goes from a mechanism, consumer need or pathway to peptide candidates, then in vitro and in vivo, in about two years on average from first hypothesis to result [00:20].
- The three things Miguel asked the audience to remember: artificial intelligence, a plant-based source, and the bioactivity of peptides [00:20].
- Potential effects the speaker listed: better glucose uptake, lower cortisol, more muscle protein, less inflammation [03:56].
- Keeping muscles healthy was linked to a 17% decrease in all-cause mortality [03:56].
- Murine hind-limb unloading: PeptiStrong prevented 50% of the muscle loss and decreased a chronic inflammatory response; Nuritas published this before going into human trials [03:56].
- The first trial was in males only; every trial since includes men and women [03:56].
- Damage trial (Dublin sports clinic, trained population, 14 days' supplementation, muscle damage induced with an isokinetic dynamometer, strength retested two and three days later): strength above baseline within two and a half weeks, with better performance from the first to the third set; irisin rose after the damaging event then returned to normal, and myostatin was tracked [03:56].
- KGK trial design: gym-familiar but untrained people, DEXA and a supervised, progressive exercise protocol against placebo; strength built first, then endurance [03:56].
- Leucine and HMB: a dose of HMB and leucine that does nothing on its own, added to a dose of PeptiStrong that does nothing on its own, boosts the effect of both [03:56]. A creatine combination was argued in theory, not shown [03:56].
- A library of more than 9 million peptides; next is a sleep peptide, non-hormonal with a peripheral mechanism, formulated by Vivatis and handed out in the sampling bag [13:50].
