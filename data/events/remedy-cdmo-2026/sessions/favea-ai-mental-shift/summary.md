# Nutraceuticals Business Mental Shift: Keeping credibility and client trust while AI speeds up the evolution

## Summary
Martina Pavlová, CEO of the Czech contract manufacturer FAVEA, asked how a nutraceutical business keeps its credibility and its clients' trust while AI speeds everything up. By expertise she means understanding, not the amount of information a company holds.

She opened with a test: which of two real enquiries to Favea, a year apart, was written with AI? Then she introduced Favea: two certified sites in the Czech Republic, 210 products in active manufacture, its own Combicaps capsule technology, and ready-to-go products from a heart-health capsule to chewable and sparkling probiotic tablets.

Back to the test. The human enquiry, from September 2025, spotted that zinc and iron in the sender's own formula could not be combined, admitted she had not known, and asked for a meeting. The AI-written one, from 2026, asked for a catalogue, prices at five pack sizes, minimum orders, samples, lead times and certificates, all at once. Her verdict: perfect form is not understanding.

Her answer: critical thinking filters the flood of information down to its essence. Without it, speed rises and understanding falls. Understanding starts with wanting to understand, then questioning the assumption and adapting; inertia is the enemy. At Favea that means validating enquiries, verifying sources and quality through expertise. Her tip for Monday: when a perfect email arrives, pick up the phone.

- Information floods in from seminars, conferences and AI; critical thinking (verifying sources and context) filters it to the essence, and without the filter speed rises and understanding falls (slide 17).
- The way out: understand, "feel it = want it", question the assumption, adapt. Inertia, "repeating what I already know", is the enemy; pause before the next course or tool and ask what you need (slide 19).
- What Favea does about it: validation of enquiries (a revised internal process), verification of sources, and quality by expertise (slide 20).
- One tip for Monday: when a perfect email arrives, pick up the phone (slide 22).
