210 true R. rosea, 143 survived cultivation: 32 were not true R. rosea, 67 true rosea lines failed in the field

The salidroside paradox
Wild extract: 1.4 % salidroside. RhoSera™: 0.8 %. Yet only
RhoSera™ switched on Pax7 and MyoD in human muscle cells.
