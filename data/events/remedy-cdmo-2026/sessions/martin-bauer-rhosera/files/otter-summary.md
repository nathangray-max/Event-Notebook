## Product Overview
**Ralph** presents **Rhosera**, a botanical ingredient derived from a proprietary cultivation of **Rhodiola rosea**. The product aims to address the health gap caused by an aging population, specifically targeting "anabolic resistance," where aging muscle cells become less efficient at utilizing protein. 

**Cultivation and Quality**
*   **Martin Bauer** utilizes a controlled, integrated cultivation process developed over 10 years to identify three elite mother lines.
*   Unlike wild-harvested plants, which are endangered and prone to adulteration, this cultivated material is grown in Central Europe without pesticides.
*   The cultivated material shows significantly higher concentrations (4 to 5-fold) of key metabolites like **rhodiolene**, **glycosyl**, and **rhodiocyanide A** compared to wild variants.
*   Preclinical data indicates the ingredient upregulates specific genes, including **Pax7** (to activate stem cells) and **myod** (for differentiation), while triggering polyamine pathways.

## Clinical Results
**Ralph** shared findings from a double-blind, placebo-controlled study involving 140 untrained adults aged 55 to 75. Participants received a daily dose of 400mg of **Rhosera** alongside defined training and protein intake.

**Key Outcomes**
*   **Muscle Soreness:** Delayed onset of muscle soreness was reduced by 46% in men and 52% in women.
*   **Physical Performance:** Statistically significant improvements were recorded in chair stand tests, single leg balance, bench press, and leg press.
*   **Longevity Biomarkers:** Improvements were noted in hand grip strength, a critical biomarker for longevity.
*   **Mental Well-being:** Participants reported reduced fatigue, improved quality of life, and increased motivation/social engagement.

## Formulation Guidance
For manufacturers looking to incorporate **Rhosera**, **Ralph** provided several technical recommendations and compliance notes.

**Usage Details**
*   Recommended dosage: 250mg to 350mg per formula.
*   Compatible formats: Tablets, capsules, bars, and V caps.
