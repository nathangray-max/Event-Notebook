# Tantus: longevity in action, from a trend to a product

## Summary
Victoria and Mateus of Tantus, a Polish CDMO, argued that a longevity trend changes nothing until it becomes a product people take every day [00:31]. They set out lifespan against healthspan and said the right question is what makes sense in a formula, not what else can be added [01:05]. Simplicity, format, dosage and ease of use sit beside the ingredient, because longevity is "a marathon" and formulations fail when people will not take them consistently [02:09]. Their process is four steps, trend, ingredient, formula, market, with suppliers treated as partners who bring science and formulation knowledge rather than as vendors [03:09]. The one concept reached before the recording ended was a chewable tablet developed with Solabia combining three grams of creatine with "400 grams" of a hesperidin-based ingredient (Otter's summary reads this as 400 mg), positioned for keeping physical function with age: "Chew it, don't shake it" [04:45].

## Key takeaways
- A trend does not change anything on its own; the product has to reach the market and become part of a daily routine [00:31].
- Consumers want to live better, not only longer: healthspan, not lifespan, drives the longevity conversation [01:05].
- The question is not what else to add to a formula but what makes sense and why [01:05].
- Tantus's principle is simplicity: clear formulation, no longer ingredient lists for their own sake; format, dosage and ease of use matter because consistent use is the point [02:09].
- Four steps: trend, ingredient (science and rationale, not the marketing story), formula (dosage, delivery form, compatibility), market (make it first, then show clients) [03:09].
- Tantus wants long-term supplier relationships rather than transactional orders, for access to research, technical information, formulation expertise and new ideas [03:09].
- Concept one: creatine reframed for longevity, a chewable tablet with Solabia, three grams of creatine with "400 grams" of a hesperidin-based ingredient (Otter's summary gives 400 mg), tried at Solabia's stand [04:45].
- Three concepts were promised; the recording ends after the first (my reading: the other two were not captured).
- The host of the previous talk introduced Tantus as a Polish contract manufacturer of tablets and hard capsules with formulation, development and some packaging (my reading, from the Solabia session's transcript).
