L I F E S P A N  V S  H E A L T H S P A N
Living well for longer
Lifespan vs. Healthspan
Lifespan counts the total number of years we live. Healthspan defines the years
spent active, capable, and cognitively well.
Beyond Cosmetic Anti-Ageing
The market opportunity lies in shifting from vague 'anti-ageing' promises toward
tangible, everyday functional preservation.
Preserving Quality of Life
True longevity is measured by sustained mobility, mental clarity,
and independence across the entire adult lifespan.

The Over-Formulation Trap
The market reflexively piles on ingredients, stacking theoretical pathways into
overly complex formulas.
The Real Formulation Question
The question is never 'what else can we add?' but 'what genuinely makes
biological sense in this specific product?'

its daily place in consumer routine.

T A N T U S   ·   L O N G E V I T Y
K E Y  T A K E A W A Y S
Keep it simple Make it last
Longevity becomes a product when it fits real life - and stays there.
01
Start with the problem, not the
ingredient count.
Avoid formulation inflation. Target
validated physiological bottlenecks that
matter for everyday vitality.
02
Choose evidence, focus, and
repeatable formats.
A supplement has no value if left in the
cabinet. Sensory experience and
convenient delivery dictate adherence.
03
Anchor innovation in durable
partnership.
Collaborate with research -driven suppliers
who share dossiers, technology, and
commitment to scientific credibility.
Good ingredients. Thoughtful products. Partnerships built for the long term.
