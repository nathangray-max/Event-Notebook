# Novonesis and Montefarmaco: the Lactoflorene partnership, from science to consumer product

## Summary
Ariel Annoni runs Novonesis's human health business across Central and Eastern Europe, the Middle East and Africa. Her argument was that a successful probiotic product needs the right science, quality, manufacturing partner and brand strategy, not just a good ingredient.

She introduced Novonesis, formed from Novozymes and Chr. Hansen, with a broad portfolio and some of the most documented strains in the world. Science is strain specific, she said, and documented strains protect the reputation of the brand a manufacturer makes. The European probiotic market is growing beyond the gut, consumers read labels and expect evidence, and probiotics are no longer a niche.

Alessia of Montefarmaco, an Italian family pharmaceutical company, then told the Lactoflorene story as the proof. The partnership began in the early 2000s with an Italian requirement for a high count in a small capsule. Novonesis supplied the potency and stability, the core formula has barely changed in almost 20 years, and Montefarmaco ran a clinical study on the finished product. Lactoflorene is now third in Italy.

Their shared conclusion: a probiotic brand is built through a long-term partnership, not one ingredient. The chair closed by saying both morning talks came back to the consumer asking "what's in it for me".

## Key takeaways
- Novonesis pitches to CMOs and CDMOs directly: probiotic solutions "already supported by research, market experience, and technical know-how", with the aim of a product the consumer will trust and repurchase, not only an ingredient sale [00:42].
- Science is strain specific; some strains have limited documentation, others decades of study, and documented strains protect the reputation of the brand the manufacturer makes [00:42].
- Four probiotic trends: more specialised needs, professional-recommended probiotics, applications beyond gut health, digital channels and emerging markets; "probiotic are no longer a niche category" [00:42].
- Lactoflorene: partnership since the early 2000s, started by an Italian requirement of at least 1 billion CFU in a limited-volume cap; Novonesis gave the potency and stability; core formula unchanged for almost 20 years; a clinical study on the finished product beyond what Italy demanded [09:06].
- Lactoflorene is third in Italy with around a 7% share; the three reasons given were clinical evidence, delivery innovation (the MCAP system for liquid shots) and formats for different ages and occasions [09:06].
- The partnership's value beyond ingredients: formulation expertise, consumer and marketing support, access to a pipeline of new bio-solutions [13:10].

## For Vita Actives
- Medium relevance: a probiotic case, but the partnership model is the one our branded ingredients compete against for a formulator's attention.
