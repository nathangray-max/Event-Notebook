# Novonesis and Montefarmaco: the Lactoflorene partnership, from science to consumer product

## Summary
Ariel Annoni, who runs Novonesis's human health business across Central and Eastern Europe, the Middle East and Africa, argued that a successful probiotic product needs the right science, quality, manufacturing partner and brand strategy, not just a good ingredient [00:42]. She introduced Novonesis as the combination of Novozymes and Chr. Hansen with more than 100 years of expertise, a portfolio of probiotics, postbiotics, enzymes, vitamins and HMOs, and strains (Otter heard "GGM BB 12") that she called among the most documented in the world [00:42]. She said the European probiotic market is growing in digestive, immune and women's health and healthy ageing, and named four trends: more specialised needs, professional-recommended probiotics, applications beyond gut health, and growth from digital channels and emerging markets [00:42]. Alessia of Montefarmaco, an Italian family pharmaceutical company founded in 1945, told the Lactoflorene story: a partnership with Novonesis since the early 2000s that began with an Italian requirement for at least 1 billion CFU in a volume-limited cap, a core formula unchanged for almost 20 years, a clinical study on the finished product, and today third place in Italy with a share of around 7% [09:06]. The chair closed by saying both morning talks came back to the consumer asking "what's in it for me" [14:17].

## Key takeaways
- Novonesis pitches to CMOs and CDMOs directly: probiotic solutions "already supported by research, market experience, and technical know-how", with the aim of a product the consumer will trust and repurchase, not only an ingredient sale [00:42].
- Science is strain specific; some strains have limited documentation, others decades of study, and documented strains protect the reputation of the brand the manufacturer makes [00:42].
- Growth areas named: digestive health, immune health, women's health, healthy ageing; new categories cardiovascular and mental health [00:42].
- Four probiotic trends: more specialised needs, professional-recommended probiotics, applications beyond gut health, digital channels and emerging markets; "probiotic are no longer a niche category" [00:42].
- Consumers are more informed, read labels and expect evidence, so quality and documentation matter more than ever [00:42].
- Lactoflorene: partnership since the early 2000s, started by an Italian requirement of at least 1 billion CFU in a limited-volume cap; Novonesis gave the potency and stability; core formula unchanged for almost 20 years; a clinical study on the finished product beyond what Italy demanded [09:06].
- Lactoflorene is third in Italy with around a 7% share; the three reasons given were clinical evidence, delivery innovation (the MCAP system for liquid shots) and formats for different ages and occasions [09:06].
- The partnership's value beyond ingredients: formulation expertise, consumer and marketing support, access to a pipeline of new bio-solutions [13:10].
- The chair's point: it is the audience and the consumer who ask "What's in it for me?" [14:17].

## For Vita Actives
Medium relevance: a probiotic case, but the partnership model is the one our branded ingredients compete against for a formulator's attention. My reading: a supplier that brings formulation help, consumer insight and a finished-product study, and keeps a formula stable for 20 years, is what a brand means by a partner; a CreaWin or IsoClr: Pea pitch to a brand or CDMO should offer the same (application work, stability, a finished-product story). My reading: Novonesis selling to CDMOs as a route to brands is the channel this conference exists for, and Polish CDMOs are a route for our sports ingredients too. Lactoflorene sells through Italian pharmacies, not our channel. No protein, creatine or sports nutrition was mentioned.
