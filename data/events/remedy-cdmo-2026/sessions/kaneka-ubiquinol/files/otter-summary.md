## Product Overview
**Ubiquinol** is the active, reduced form of **Coenzyme Q10**, which provides direct benefits to the body without requiring conversion by the enzyme diaphorase—an enzyme that decreases with age. 
* **Biological functions**: Acts as a cofactor for cellular energy, an antioxidant, and supports mitochondrial biogenesis.
* **Key benefits**: Supports heart, immune, reproductive, and cognitive health, as well as healthy aging and menopause.
* **Bioavailability comparison**: Blood tests show that 90 mg of **Ubiquinol** provides an equivalent blood level to 300 mg of **Coenzyme Q10**. High doses of **Coenzyme Q10** (above 300 mg) are often ineffective due to limited conversion capacity.
