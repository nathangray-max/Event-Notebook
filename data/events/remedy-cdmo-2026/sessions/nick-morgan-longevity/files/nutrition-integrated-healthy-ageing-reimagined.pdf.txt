Grounding the conversation
Healthy Ageing
Healthy ageing is the process of
developing & maintaining functional
ability that enables wellbeing as we age.
How do we maintain wellbeing & function
with age?
Healthspan
The period of life spent in good health,
free from chronic disease, disability, or
substantial functional impairment.
How long do we remain healthy and
functional?
Longevity
Longevity is focused with the duration
of life, not necessarily the quality of life.
Simply, the number of life years.
How long do we live?

--- page 4 ---
To alter, optimise or personalise
01
Altering the ageing
process
Focus on the biological processes
associated with ageing (hallmarks)
Examples: Cellular health,
mitochondrial health, cellular energy,
senescence, metabolic signalling.
The starting point is the biology of ageing
02
Optimising the ageing
experience
Preserve the physical, cognitive
and emotional capabilities people
value for longer.
Examples: Muscle & mobility, cognition,
energy, sleep, gut, metabolic health,
beauty, immunity.
Priorities vary by consumer and stage.
03
Personalising the
age focus
Translate longevity into the needs
of a particular consumer, lifestyle or
life stage.
Examples: Ageing athletes, women
through menopause and later life,
beauty longevity.
The starting point is the consumer context.
