# Nick Morgan: how longevity is sold, from hallmarks to habits

## Summary
The recording catches the last six minutes of Nick Morgan's talk on the longevity consumer. He argued that longevity now sits inside women's health, menopause and sports nutrition, and that consumers connect with "energy and vitality" and "cellular health" rather than the hallmarks of ageing: of about 300 products coded, 9% refer to the hallmarks and, he thinks, only 4% put them in lead copy [00:00]. Brands sell routines, not single products; Solero's AM, PM and two-days-a-month reset was his example [00:00]. Longevity is one of the most expensive categories per serve, and an existing ingredient such as creatine, at a median of around 50 cents a serve, can be made premium by putting longevity on the front [00:00]. Formats are moving beyond capsules (70% of products) into liquids, chocolate, gummies, an NAD energy drink and a spermidine pen, and longevity is moving into hydration and sports nutrition, with Sergio Ramos's brand Centenarian as the example [00:00].

## Key takeaways
- Consumers buy "energy and vitality" and "cellular health"; the hallmarks of ageing are "for this people in this room" [00:00].
- Of about 300 products coded, 9% refer to the hallmarks and only 4%, he thinks, put them in lead copy [00:00].
- "You don't sell one product in 2026": brands sell a routine of about three products, and an ingredient is sold on how it fits the customer's product system [00:00].
- Longevity products are among the most expensive per serve; the creatine median is about 50 cents a serve, and the same ingredient labelled longevity can be "a hell of a lot more expensive" [00:00].
- 70% of longevity products are capsules, but liquids, chocolate, gummies, drinks and injection pens are arriving [00:00].
- Cellular claims are entering hydration, and longevity is shifting into sports nutrition (Centenarian, linked to Sergio Ramos) [00:00].
- Among the ingredients on the longevity shelf he named resveratrol, CoQ10, ubiquinol and spermidine [00:00].

## For Vita Actives
