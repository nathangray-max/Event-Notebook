## Marketing Strategy

* **Microbiomex**, a flavonol with prebiotic effects, was rebranded as **Copilot Freedom Probiotic** because "flavorbiotic" was too difficult for consumers to understand and remember.

## Manufacturing Challenges
Successful products require balancing complex ingredient lists with physical stability and delivery formats. 
* Creating products with high doses of multiple ingredients (e.g., 3–5g of creatine or 5–10g of collagen) can lead to issues with solubility and stomach mixing.
* **Thomas** highlighted a partnership with the CDMO **Tantus** to solve creatine degradation in gummies caused by heat application. By using a cold-pressed tablet method combined with **WhatsApp** (a citrus extract), they maintain the integrity of the creatine while supporting nitric oxide production and blood flow.
