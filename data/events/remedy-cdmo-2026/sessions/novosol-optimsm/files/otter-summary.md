This meeting covers presentations on **NovoSol** technology and the nutritional benefits of **OptiMSM**.

## Technology Overview
**Toshton Sack** presented the **NovoSol** micellar encapsulation technology, which addresses the challenge of limited solubility in lipophilic ingredients.
* **NovoSol** uses a biomimetic delivery system to create a water-soluble, homogeneous liquid concentrate that protects ingredients from stomach acid.
* The process is physical rather than chemical, meaning the identity of the active ingredient remains unchanged.
* Key products include **NovoSol Curcumin**, **NovoSol Astaxanthin**, and **NovoSol Coenzyme Q10**.
* Applications include capsules, liquid shots, and "cap and cap" systems like the **Nourreflex PD** by **Favea**, which separates ingredients to reduce interaction risks.

## Nutritional Science
**Lena Leda** discussed the research regarding **OptiMSM** (methylsulfonylmethane) and its role in human nutrition.
* **OptiMSM** is a sulfur-based nutrient composed of 34% sulfur by weight, oxygen, and two methyl groups.
* The molecule serves structural roles by supporting the synthesis of critical molecules such as glutathione (an antioxidant), keratin, and cartilage.
* Clinical benefits identified include support for joint health, skin health, and exercise recovery.
* **Balcom** manages a broad portfolio including chelated minerals, choline, vitamin K2, and methylfolate.
