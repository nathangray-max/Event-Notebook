# Aquanova NovaSol micellar delivery, then Balchem OptiMSM

## Summary
Two short talks in one recording.

Torsten Seck, director of sales and marketing at Aquanova, asked how delivery technology can turn a hard-to-use, fat-soluble ingredient into a market-ready, differentiated product. His answer was NovaSOL, a micellar technology. The native ingredient sits in a tiny micelle with a fat-loving core and a water-loving shell, so it dissolves in water and passes stomach acid. Processing is physical, with no chemical change, and each formula is unique to its ingredient. The result is a liquid concentrate ready for capsules, shots and combinations. His examples were Favea's cap-in-cap product, NeuroFlex PD, and the US longevity brand Nugevia, which built three products on the same micellar resveratrol. His conclusion: NovaSOL is a bridge between delivery science and differentiated finished products.

Lena Leder, senior manager nutrition science at Balchem, then began on OptiMSM. She set it in Balchem's 33 active research projects, then described MSM as a source of sulfur and methyl groups which, she said, has shown effects on joint and skin health and exercise recovery. The recording ends about three minutes into her talk, before the data.

## Key takeaways
- NovaSOL is a micelle encapsulation technology: the active ingredient is the core, a food-grade liquid emulsification system the shell; it is water-soluble and made by physical processing only, with no chemical modification (slide 3) (slide 4) [00:18].
- Each NovaSOL formula is unique to its ingredient; it is a water-free liquid concentrate for capsules and shots, and different NovaSOL formulations can be combined in capsules (slide 3) (slide 5).
- Nugevia, a US direct-to-consumer brand, launched three combination products on Jotrol micellar resveratrol: MND with NovaSOL Curcumin, PWR with NovaSOL Q and GLO with NovaSOL Astaxanthin, in vegetarian SeaGel softgels, positioned in longevity (slide 7).
- MSM is 34% sulfur by weight, a source of sulfur and methyl groups; supplementation "has shown" effects on joint and skin health and exercise recovery, the speaker said [10:40].
- Balchem has 33 active research projects; methylfolate is a new ingredient it hopes to bring to Europe soon [09:04].
