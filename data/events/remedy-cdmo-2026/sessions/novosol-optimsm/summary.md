# Aquanova NovaSol micellar delivery, then Balchem OptiMSM

## Summary
Two short talks in one recording.

Aquanova's speaker asked how delivery technology can turn a hard-to-use, fat-soluble ingredient into a market-ready, differentiated product. His answer was NovaSol, a micellar technology. The native ingredient sits in a tiny micelle with a fat-loving core and a water-loving surface, so it dissolves in water and passes stomach acid. Processing is physical, with no chemical change, and each formulation is unique to its ingredient. The result is a liquid concentrate ready for capsules, shots and combinations. His examples were Favea's cap-in-cap product, Nourreflex PD, and the US longevity brand GVR, which built three products on the same micellar resveratrol. His conclusion: NovaSol is a bridge between delivery science and differentiated finished products.

Lena Leder, senior manager nutrition science at Balchem, then began on OptiMSM. She set it in Balchem's 33 active research projects, then described MSM as a source of sulfur and methyl groups which, she said, has shown effects on joint and skin health and exercise recovery. The recording ends about three minutes into her talk, before the data.

## Key takeaways
- NovaSol is a micellar technology: lipophilic core, hydrophilic surface, water-soluble, protected from stomach acid, with no chemical change to the active [00:18].
- Each NovaSol formulation is unique to its ingredient; it is a liquid concentrate for capsules, shots and combined capsules [00:18].
- GVR, a US direct-to-consumer longevity brand, built Mind, Q Power and Glow on the same micellar resveratrol with different NovaSol actives, in vegetarian softgels [00:18].
- MSM is 34% sulfur by weight, a source of sulfur and methyl groups; supplementation "has shown" effects on joint and skin health and exercise recovery, the speaker said [10:40].
- Balchem has 33 active research projects; methylfolate is a new ingredient it hopes to bring to Europe soon [09:04].
