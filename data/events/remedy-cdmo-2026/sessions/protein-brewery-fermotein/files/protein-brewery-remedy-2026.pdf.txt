SCIENCE:
FERMOTEIN® bioactives extend lifespan in relevant model of ageing
+21% median lifespan vs. control diet
C. elegans survival on the water-soluble fraction of FERMOTEIN® (229.6 mg/mL, n=137) against
standard diet (n=102). Median lifespan 28 vs. 23 days, p<0.0001 (log-rank).
1 Soluble fraction of FERMOTEIN®
2 Dose equivalence is indicative only. Intake cannot be translated directly between model organisms and humans, so the ~8 g figure is given for orientation and is not a recommended human intake.
Source: NemaLife Inc., C. elegans lifespan study, 2026. Preclinical research for product development purposes; not a health claim.
C. elegans, the gold-standard model
C. elegans has been the model organism in lifespan science for over
50 years, with core ageing pathways conserved in humans.
Independent, credentialed science
Run by NemaLife, a Texas Tech University spin-off founded by
Prof. Siva Vanapalli PhD, Regents Chair in Engineering and
author of 100+ peer-reviewed papers.
Unique FERMOTEIN® bioactives
The nematodes received the water-soluble fraction of
FERMOTEIN®: spermidine plus further bioactives unique to our
strain, now under research. Dose equivalent: 14 mg spermidine,
or ~8 g of FERMOTEIN®.2
