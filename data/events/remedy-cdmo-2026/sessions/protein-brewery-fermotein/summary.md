# The Protein Brewery: Fermotein, a fermented mycelium protein for active and all-in-one nutrition

## Summary
Thijs Bos, CEO of The Protein Brewery and formerly a seller of dairy and fava proteins, presented Fermotein, a mycelium ingredient grown by biomass fermentation in Breda, in the Netherlands. His pitch was that one ingredient can carry protein, fibre and longevity bioactives together.

He said Fermotein is 50% complete protein and 30% prebiotic fibre, and is rich in spermidine and choline. It is the first mycelium protein with EFSA approval, which came this summer after five years. While waiting, the company built its market in the US, in "all-in-one nutrition": powders that sit between protein powder and meal replacement, with Ka'Chava as the model. Superfood powders are adding protein and fibre too. In Europe meal replacement is still the nearest category, and he asked the CDMOs in the room to embrace the all-in-one concept.

On evidence he cited prebiotic studies, a positive effect on GLP-1, and a nematode study in which Fermotein extended lifespan, with a human trial on a biological ageing clock planned with Wageningen University.

He closed on scale: 10 million servings sold in 2026, three US customers launched, and 70 million raised, with Novo Holdings the largest shareholder. The company is not yet profitable.
