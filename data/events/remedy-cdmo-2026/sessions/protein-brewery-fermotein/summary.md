# The Protein Brewery: Fermotein, a fermented mycelium protein for active and all-in-one nutrition

## Summary
Thijs Bos, CEO of The Protein Brewery (Breda, Netherlands, founded 2020), presented Fermotein, a mycelium ingredient grown by biomass fermentation in two 85,000 litre fermenters [00:00]. He said it is 50% complete protein and 30% prebiotic fibre (chitin and chitosan), with spermidine at about 1700 mg per kilo (seven times beet germ, as said) and choline, all in one ingredient aimed at active nutrition. It is the first mycelium protein with EFSA approval, "since the summer of this year" after five years; the company built its market in the US all-in-one nutrition segment, which sits between protein powder and meal replacement, with Ka'Chava as the example, and he expects Europe to follow. Evidence he cited: prebiotic studies (short-chain fatty acids, alpha diversity), a positive effect on GLP-1, and a C. elegans study in which Fermotein increased lifespan by 22%, with a follow-up and a human biological ageing clock trial with Wageningen University planned for quarter one. Commercially: 10 million servings sold in 2026, three US customers, 70 million raised over three rounds with Novo Holdings the largest shareholder, approval in Singapore, and Australia and Canada pending.
