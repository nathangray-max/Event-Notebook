# From cell to scale: Patented strains & custom fermentation power

## Summary
Sacco System's speaker presented the company as a family-owned probiotic maker and CDMO that sells speed and flexibility. The recording joins mid-introduction, and the slides do not name him. Sacco dates from 1872 and turned over €195 million in 2025.

He described its offer: probiotics, postbiotics and tailor-made mixtures, strains screened to EFSA safety requirements, custom fermentation at any scale, and next-generation strains developed for third parties. Sacco has recently invested in clinical studies and patents in two areas, women's health and healthy ageing, and he gave one strain for each.

CA15, a patented strain isolated from a healthy woman, was tested in two placebo-controlled trials in women with vaginal imbalance. He reported fewer symptoms after ten days, held a month after intake stopped.

Synbio, a patented mix of two strains isolated from elderly people in Marche, was tested over six months in 97 healthy people over 65. He reported more beneficial bacteria and butyrate, a 43% fall in a marker of inflammation, higher IGF-1, which he linked to muscle maintenance, and slower shortening of telomeres.

His close was an invitation: Sacco will take on unusual projects quickly and work closely with the customer.

## Key takeaways
- Its pitch as a CDMO is speed and flexibility: a low time to market, agile family decisions, custom fermentation from 1 L to 40,000 L, and a new kind of production if asked (slide 6) (slide 8) [00:00].
- CA15 trials: two randomised, double-blind, placebo-controlled studies, 60 women in 2023 and 200 in 2025, one capsule of 10 billion CFU a day for 10 days and a 30-day wash-out (slide 14). They differ from the talk, which gave a six-woman exploratory study and "10 mil per capsule" [00:00].
- CA15 results: symptom scores fell 78% for discomfort, 66% burning, 64% itching, 62% secretions and 56% inflammation (slide 15). On the Amsel criteria 98% of women had a corrected pH, 92% a negative amine test, 79% no clue cells and 75% normal discharge, as labelled on the chart (slide 16); he gave the first three the same [00:00].
- Synbio trial: L. rhamnosus IMC 501 and L. paracasei IMC 502, 5 billion CFU a serving for six months in 97 healthy people over 65, randomised, double-blind and placebo-controlled (slide 21) (slide 23); spoken as "five mil" daily [00:00].
- Synbio results, end of study against start: hs-CRP down 43%, IL-8 down 83%, IL-6 67%, MIP-1α 60% and TNF-α 23% (slide 25); total short-chain fatty acids up 23% and butyric acid up 33% (slide 26) [00:00].
- Sacco says Synbio "increases IGF-1 levels", which it calls key for muscle maintenance in ageing, and that it slowed telomere shortening against placebo (slide 27) (slide 28) (slide 29) [00:00].

## Detail
- The deck is titled "From cell to scale: Patented strains & custom fermentation power", subtitled "Targeted solutions for healthy ageing & women's health" (slide 2). No slide names the speaker. No slide is marked confidential.
- Sacco's numbers: more than 6,500 strains in its bank, more than 600 people, 11 production plants, €195 million turnover in 2025, more than 110 export countries, more than 40 university collaborations (slide 4).
- Presence: headquarters in Italy (Sacco System and CSL, Centro Sperimentale del Latte), CSL USA, and Sacco System companies in France, the Nordics, Japan, China, Australia, Brazil and Uruguay (slide 5). He named plants in Como, Milan (CSL, pharmaceutical API probiotics) and Wisconsin, and a Brisbane pilot for next-generation and exotic strains [00:00].
- Offer: CultureScience, clinically documented strains; CultureSelect, strains screened for EFSA safety and function (at least 30 species); Made For You, development of next-generation probiotics and live biotherapeutics (slide 7) [00:00].
- Manufacturing: GMP pharma plants and pilot labs, qualified persons and validation experts, religious certifications, a hypoallergenic production system (slide 6); food-grade GMP, an anaerobic pilot plant for next-generation strains, and uses in supplements, pharma, food and drinks (slide 8).
- Therapeutic areas: gut, immune, skin, healthy ageing, metabolic, sport, women's, oral, infant and children, mental health (slide 9).
- CA15 is Lacticaseibacillus rhamnosus CA15 DSM 33960, isolated in 2022 in Sicily from a healthy woman (slide 11). Otter's "in CCD" is Sicily [00:00].
- CA15 in vitro: more than 95% survival in simulated gut transit; adhesion of 36% to intestinal Caco-2 cells, 34% to vaginal VK2/E6E7 cells and 12% to intestinal mucus; produces lactic acid and hydrogen peroxide (slide 12).
- In the SHIME gut model a 10-billion capsule survived the small intestine and colon, fasted and fed, alone and with fluconazole 200 mg; the slide cites Vaccalluzzo A et al, Food Res Int (2025) (slide 13). He said the University of Catania ran the clinical studies [00:00]; the slides neither name Catania nor cite the trial papers.
- CA15 outcomes: primary, microbiota rebalance, no signs or symptoms of dysbiosis, normal Amsel and Nugent scores; secondary, quality of life (slide 14).
- Nugent score: CA15 moved women from the dysbiotic band (7 to 10) to normal (0 to 3) by day 10 and held at day 30, while placebo stayed in or near the dysbiotic band, read from the chart (slide 17).
- Vaginal counts: Lactobacillus rose from about 3.5 to about 7 log10 CFU/ml in 10 days; Enterococcus, Staphylococcus, Gardnerella, Candida and E. coli fell, and both held 30 days after the last dose, read from the chart (slide 18) [00:00].
- CA15 key messages also claim better quality of life, resistance to fluconazole, and remission "not only maintained, but further increased" (slide 19).
- Synbio strains were isolated in 2021 in Marche from elderly people (slide 21); he said the ratio is 1 to 1 and the region has one of Europe's highest shares of centenarians [00:00]. The slide says Synbio is backed by "over 50 scientific publications" and more than 1,000 people in human studies (slide 21).
- The Synbio trial slide carries a "ProBioSenior" logo. Primary outcomes were hs-CRP, gut microbiota and fatty-acid production; secondary, cytokines, IGF-1 and nutritional status (slide 23).
- Gut changes after six months: Akkermansia, Bifidobacterium, Lactobacillus and butyrate up, inflammation down (slide 24) [00:00].
- Chart values, read from the charts: hs-CRP from about 4 to 2.3 mg/l (slide 25); fatty acids from about 57 to 70 µmol/g and butyric acid from about 9 to 12 µmol/g (slide 26). Each is marked significant by t-test (P<0.05).
- IGF-1 rose from about 118 to 145 pg/ml with Synbio and from about 118 to 120 with placebo; normal nutrition on the questionnaire rose from about 28% to 57% of the Synbio group, and malnourished fell from about 18% to 7%, with placebo little changed, read from the charts (slide 27) [00:00].
- Telomere length (estimated marginal means): placebo fell from about 1.025 to 1.00 and Synbio rose from about 1.018 to 1.027 over 180 days, read from the chart; the chart carries no significance mark (slide 28). He said there was "a decrease in the reduction in the length" [00:00].
- Otter heard the mix as "Symbio" [00:00]; the slides spell it SYNBIO (slide 20). He used Gemini to find the shoelace metaphor for telomeres, which the slide repeats (slide 28) [00:00].
