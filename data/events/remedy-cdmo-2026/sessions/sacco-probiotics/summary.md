# Sacco System: probiotic CDMO services, CA15 for women's health and Symbio for healthy ageing

## Summary
Sacco System's speaker, joined mid-introduction by the recording, presented the company as a probiotic maker and CDMO that sells speed and flexibility. It is a family company founded in 1872, selling in more than 110 countries, with plants in Italy and the US and a pilot for next-generation strains in Australia.

He described its offer: probiotics, postbiotics and custom mixtures from strains screened to EFSA requirements, and a service that develops strains for third parties. Sacco has recently invested in clinical studies and patents in two areas, women's health and healthy ageing, and he gave one strain for each.

CA15, a patented strain isolated from healthy women, was tested in women with vaginal infections. He reported fewer symptoms and, one month after intake stopped, most women with a restored pH.

Symbio, a mix of two strains isolated from centenarians, was tested over six months in 97 elderly subjects. He reported more beneficial bacteria, a 43% reduction in a marker of inflammation, better IGF-1, which he linked to muscle mass, and a slower shortening of telomeres.

His close was an invitation: Sacco will take on unusual projects quickly and work closely with the customer.

## Key takeaways
- Its pitch as a CDMO is speed and flexibility: a low time to market, working in synergy with the customer, and setting up a new kind of production if asked ("If you have got something crazy, we like to do that") [00:00].
- Offer: probiotics, postbiotics and customised mixtures; "culture science" strains with clinical studies and "culture select" strains screened to EFSA requirements, more than 30 species; strain development for third parties [00:00].
- CA15 (women's health): patented strain, 2022; six-woman exploratory study then 200 women; "10 mil per capsule" for 10 days, one-month washout; he reported reduced symptoms and, one month after intake stopped, 98% restored pH and 92% negative amine test [00:00].
- Symbio (healthy ageing): 1:1 paracasei and rhamnosus from Marche centenarians; a randomised, double-blind, placebo-controlled study in 97 subjects, "five mil" daily, six months [00:00].
- Symbio results as he gave them: more lactobacilli, bifidobacteria and butyrate, a 43% reduction in hs-CRP ("a marker of inflammaging"), reduced TNF, improved IGF-1 "correlated with the protein synthesis as well as the maintenance of the muscle mass", and "a decrease in the reduction in the length" of telomeres [00:00].
- Sacco has recently invested in clinical studies and patents in two areas, women's health and healthy ageing [00:00].
