# Sacco System: probiotic CDMO services, CA15 for women's health and Symbio for healthy ageing

## Summary
The recording joins Sacco System's speaker mid-introduction. He presented Sacco as a family company founded in 1872, selling in more than 110 countries, with plants in Como and Milan (the sister company CSL, pharmaceutical API probiotics), a plant in Wisconsin and a pilot for next-generation bacteria in Brisbane [00:00]. He described GMP pharma and GMP food-grade sites, strains screened to EFSA requirements across more than 30 species, a "made-for-you" service developing strains for third parties, and a recent investment in clinical studies and patents in two areas, women's health and healthy ageing [00:00]. CA15, a patented strain isolated from healthy women in 2022, was tested by the University of Catania in an exploratory study of six women and a study of 200 women with bacterial vaginosis and vulvovaginal candidiasis, 10 days of oral intake and a one-month washout; he reported less burning, discomfort, itching, secretions and inflammation and, one month after stopping, 98% with a restored pH range and 92% with a negative amine test [00:00]. Symbio, a 1:1 mix of paracasei and rhamnosus strains isolated from centenarians in the Marche region, was tested in a randomised, double-blind, placebo-controlled study of 97 elderly subjects over six months; he reported more lactobacilli, bifidobacteria and butyrate, a 43% reduction in high-sensitivity C-reactive protein, an improvement in IGF-1, which he linked to protein synthesis and muscle mass, and a slower reduction in telomere length [00:00].

## Key takeaways
- Sacco System: family company since 1872, more than 110 countries, plants in Como, Milan (CSL, pharma API probiotics) and Wisconsin, a Brisbane pilot for next-generation and exotic strains [00:00].
- Its pitch as a CDMO is speed and flexibility: a low time to market, working in synergy with the customer, and setting up a new kind of production if asked ("If you have got something crazy, we like to do that") [00:00].
- Offer: probiotics, postbiotics and customised mixtures; "culture science" strains with clinical studies and "culture select" strains screened to EFSA requirements, more than 30 species; strain development for third parties [00:00].
- CA15 (women's health): patented strain, 2022; six-woman exploratory study then 200 women; "10 mil per capsule" for 10 days, one-month washout; he reported reduced symptoms and, one month after intake stopped, 98% restored pH and 92% negative amine test [00:00].
- He said the strain survives the gut, reaches the vagina and promotes the natural lactobacilli while reducing enterococci and Gardnerella vaginalis [00:00].
- Symbio (healthy ageing): 1:1 paracasei and rhamnosus from Marche centenarians; RCT in 97 subjects, "five mil" daily, six months [00:00].
- Symbio results as he gave them: more lactobacilli, bifidobacteria and butyrate, a 43% reduction in hs-CRP ("a marker of inflammaging"), reduced TNF, improved IGF-1 "correlated with the protein synthesis as well as the maintenance of the muscle mass", and "a decrease in the reduction in the length" of telomeres [00:00].
- He used Gemini to find the shoelace metaphor for telomeres [00:00].

## For Vita Actives
Medium relevance. Sacco is a probiotic ingredient maker and CDMO, so it shows who makes the probiotic part of our customers' products, not a buyer for ours. The one direct touch-point is the healthy-ageing pitch: Symbio was presented with a muscle-mass argument through IGF-1 and sarcopenia, which is the territory where creatine and protein are pitched to active-ageing brands. My reading: expect active-ageing launches that pair a probiotic with creatine or protein, and a CreaWin "muscle plus gut" story with a partner like Sacco is a possible co-formulation pitch. The claims are the speaker's, from one 97-subject study whose publication was not named. No protein, creatine or sports nutrition was mentioned.
