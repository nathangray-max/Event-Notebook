The meeting focused on innovative probiotic dosage forms and clinically tested probiotic strain technologies.

## Dosage Form Innovation
**Miguel** from **SPI Pharma** presented advanced delivery systems designed to improve consumer experience and stability.
*   **Composol Pro Tab**: A polyol-based, low water activity platform used to create chewable probiotic tablets with low compression forces to ensure probiotic survival.
*   **Effer Soda**: A partially transformed sodium bicarbonate product that creates a protective layer, enhancing stability in effervescent tablets, stick packs, or sachets. This technology can also produce a "delicate fizz" in fast-melting solutions to mask bitterness.
*   **Orally Disintegrating Tablets (ODT)**: Fast-melting platforms designed for populations with dysphagia, such as children and the elderly, allowing for consumption without water.

## Probiotic Research
**Mihail Jakubowski** of **Nordic Biotic** detailed scientific advancements in specialized probiotic strains and their metabolic functions.
*   **Northbiotic Mental Balance**: Developed with the **Polish Academy of Sciences**, this blend uses **Lactobacillus plantarum 140** (producing GABA) and **Bifidobacterium breve 010** (producing vitamin B free). An eight-week study of 227 students demonstrated statistical efficacy in improving sleep quality and managing stress.
*   **Microplastic Management**: A novel approach using two specific strains for gut cleansing:
    1.  **Lactobacillus acidophilus 120**: Aggregates and binds microplastic particles.
    2.  **Lactobacillus fermentum 160**: Pushes microplastics away from the mucosal lining to facilitate removal.
*   Human studies regarding the microplastic-binding technology are currently in progress.
