# SPI Pharma and Nordic Biotic: new dosage forms and clinically tested strains

## Summary
The recording joins Miguel of SPI Pharma mid-talk. He showed three dosage-form concepts: chewable probiotic tablets on a polyol-based, low-water-activity platform (heard as "composol pro tab") compressed at low force so the probiotics survive, with a live product shown under Favea's branding [00:01]; effervescent hydration formats (tablets, stick packs, sachets) stabilised with Effer Soda, a sodium bicarbonate partly transformed to carbonate to form a protective layer, which can also give a "delicate fizz" in chewables and fast melts that distracts the tongue from bitterness [00:01]; and fast-melt (orally disintegrating) tablets for children, elderly people with dysphagia and anyone who dislikes swallowing [00:01]. His close, aimed at the capsule maker ACG: "don't ask your consumer to swallow another capsule" [07:50]. Mihail Jakubowski of Nordic Biotic (a silver sponsor) said the company supplies clinically tested probiotics to brand owners, CDMOs and distributors with full exclusivity by territory at the strain level, and works from genomics to metabolomics (630 molecules measured), functional testing and human studies [09:25]. He presented Northbiotic Mental Balance (Lactobacillus plantarum 140 producing GABA and Bifidobacterium breve 010 producing a B vitamin), tested for eight weeks in 227 students at exam time, 219 completing, with results received the week before; he said statistical efficacy was proved for sleep quality and stress [09:25]. He also showed a microplastic blend of Lactobacillus acidophilus 120 (binds) and Lactobacillus fermentum 160 (pushes away), with a human study in progress and an invitation to a "plastic gut-free initiative" [09:25].

## Key takeaways
- Chewable probiotic tablets: a polyol-based platform with low water content and water activity, compressed at low force, sweet without sugar; probiotics survive the process; shown in a real Favea product [00:01].
- Hydration is a trend in supplements and beverages; effervescence relies on sodium bicarbonate, which with an acid is hygroscopic and unstable over shelf life; Effer Soda is SPI Pharma's stabilised form for tablets, stick packs and sachets [00:01].
- A delicate fizz in chewables or fast melts is also a way to distract the tongue from bitterness [00:01].
- Fast-melt tablets need no water and suit children, elderly people with dysphagia and people who dislike swallowing; SPI Pharma offers an ODT platform that can carry fizz [00:01].
- SPI Pharma's role, as he put it, is creating product concepts so a CDMO can expand into new dosage forms; "we do not limit there" [00:01].
- Nordic Biotic: full exclusivity by territory at strain level; genomics, then metabolomics (630 molecules, "the postbiotics actually"), then functional testing, then human studies; CFU measured to milligrams [09:25].
- Mental Balance: strains chosen for what they produce (L. plantarum 140, GABA; B. breve 010, a B vitamin); eight-week study, 227 students started, 219 completed, "one of the largest study in stress in probiotics in the last decade"; results received last week; he said statistical efficacy was proved in sleep quality and stress [09:25].
- Microplastic blend: L. acidophilus 120 aggregates microplastic, L. fermentum 160 pushes it away; offered as a standalone or an add-on to a gut, detox or clean-label formula; the human study is in progress [09:25].
- Nordic Biotic has a GLP-1 companion product with its Swiss sister company Azento; gut-skin, gut-bone and gut-brain technologies were named [09:25].
- WIPO innovation index as he cited it: of 12 countries, seven are European; in the region Latvia, Czechia, Hungary and Poland lead [09:25].
- The chair's aside: Coca-Cola was first sold as a medicinal tonic for brain and nerves [08:30].

## For Vita Actives
Medium relevance, the highest of the four: formats, not ingredients. Hydration sticks, effervescent tablets, chewables and fast melts are the formats our sports and active nutrition customers sell, and SPI Pharma's platforms are what a CDMO uses to make them. My reading: a CreaWin chewable or fast-melt tablet, and creatine or electrolytes in a stable effervescent stick, are format questions to raise with the CDMOs who work with SPI Pharma and Favea; Favea (shown making a live product) is a CDMO to know. My reading: Nordic Biotic's GLP-1 companion product is the adjacent opportunity for IsoClr: Pea, since protein for muscle preservation on GLP-1 drugs is a brief our customers already have; its stress and sleep blend sits next to recovery positioning in active nutrition. Nordic Biotic's exclusivity-by-territory model is a pattern a branded ingredient supplier can study (my reading). All efficacy claims are the speakers' and the Mental Balance study is unpublished as of the talk.
