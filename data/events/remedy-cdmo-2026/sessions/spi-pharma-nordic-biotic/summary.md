# SPI Pharma and Nordic Biotic: new dosage forms and clinically tested strains

## Summary
Two suppliers shared this slot. Miguel of SPI Pharma, joined mid-talk by the recording, argued that CDMOs can grow by moving probiotics and other actives into new dosage forms, and showed three concepts.

The first was a chewable probiotic tablet, made on a low-moisture platform and pressed gently so the bacteria survive. The second was effervescent hydration formats, kept stable with a modified sodium bicarbonate; a light fizz also helps mask bitterness. The third was fast-melt tablets for children, older people who struggle to swallow, and anyone who dislikes pills. His close, aimed at the capsule maker ACG: do not ask consumers to swallow another capsule.

Mihail Jakubowski of Nordic Biotic then presented a supplier of clinically tested probiotic strains, sold with exclusivity by territory. Its work runs from genomics and metabolomics to human studies. He showed a stress and sleep blend tested on 227 students at exam time, saying the results, received the week before, proved efficacy on sleep quality and stress. He also showed a blend aimed at microplastics, with its human study still in progress.

His close was an invitation to develop new products together.

## Key takeaways
- Chewable probiotic tablets: a polyol-based platform with low water content and water activity, compressed at low force, sweet without sugar; probiotics survive the process; shown in a real Favea product [00:01].
- Effer Soda is SPI Pharma's stabilised sodium bicarbonate for effervescent tablets, stick packs and sachets; plain bicarbonate with an acid is hygroscopic and unstable over shelf life [00:01].
- Fast-melt tablets need no water and suit children, elderly people with dysphagia and people who dislike swallowing; SPI Pharma offers an ODT platform that can carry fizz [00:01].
- Nordic Biotic: full exclusivity by territory at strain level; genomics, then metabolomics (630 molecules, "the postbiotics actually"), then functional testing, then human studies [09:25].
- Mental Balance: eight-week study, 227 students started, 219 completed, "one of the largest study in stress in probiotics in the last decade"; results received last week; he said statistical efficacy was proved in sleep quality and stress [09:25].
- Nordic Biotic has a GLP-1 companion product with its Swiss sister company Azento [09:25].

## For Vita Actives
- Medium relevance, the highest of the four: formats, not ingredients. Hydration sticks, effervescent tablets, chewables and fast melts are the formats our sports and active nutrition customers sell, and SPI Pharma's platforms are what a CDMO uses to make them.

## Detail
- The chewable platform was heard as "composol pro tab"; the live product was shown under Favea's branding [00:01].
- Hydration is a trend in supplements and beverages. Effer Soda is a sodium bicarbonate partly transformed to carbonate to form a protective layer [00:01].
- A "delicate fizz" in chewables or fast melts is also a way to distract the tongue from bitterness [00:01].
- SPI Pharma's role, as he put it, is creating product concepts so a CDMO can expand into new dosage forms; "we do not limit there" [00:01].
- Nordic Biotic, a silver sponsor, supplies clinically tested probiotics to brand owners, CDMOs and distributors; CFU is measured to milligrams [09:25].
- Mental Balance (Northbiotic Mental Balance): strains chosen for what they produce, Lactobacillus plantarum 140 (GABA) and Bifidobacterium breve 010 (a B vitamin); tested in students at exam time [09:25].
- Microplastic blend: Lactobacillus acidophilus 120 aggregates (binds) microplastic, Lactobacillus fermentum 160 pushes it away; offered as a standalone or an add-on to a gut, detox or clean-label formula; human study in progress, with an invitation to a "plastic gut-free initiative" [09:25].
- Gut-skin, gut-bone and gut-brain technologies were named [09:25].
- WIPO innovation index as he cited it: of 12 countries, seven are European; in the region Latvia, Czechia, Hungary and Poland lead [09:25].
- His closing line: "We will not proudly invent the the next wheel in the next week, but I'm pretty sure we can create something new together." [09:25]
