# SPI Pharma and Nordic Biotic: new dosage forms and clinically tested strains

## Summary
Two suppliers shared this slot. Miguel of SPI Pharma, an ABF Ingredients company, presented "From platform to product". His case: consumers buy formats first, so the gap is not another active but a format people will take. Last year SPI Pharma showed probiotics can be tableted. This year it showed what that tablet becomes.

He showed three concepts, each on an SPI platform. A mint chewable probiotic for adults, pressed gently on a low-moisture platform so the cultures survive. A fizzy electrolyte and energy stick for sport, kept stable by a modified sodium bicarbonate; a light fizz also masks bitterness. A vanilla fast-melt for healthy ageing that needs no water. The packs were illustrative concepts. His close: do not ask the consumer to swallow another capsule. The deck also asked the room for ideas, as SPI Pharma looks to grow in nutrition.

Mihail Jakubowski of Nordic Biotic then presented a supplier of clinically tested probiotic strains, sold with exclusivity by territory. He showed a stress and sleep blend tested on 227 students at exam time, saying the results, received the week before, proved efficacy on sleep quality and stress. He also showed a blend aimed at microplastics, with its human study still in progress. He closed with an invitation to develop products together.

## Key takeaways
- Chewable probiotic: Compressol ProTab is a low water activity direct-compression platform that compresses at low force, so live cultures survive; the concept is an adult mint chew, "Gut Performance Chew" (slide 5) (slide 7) [00:01].
- Effer-Soda is a surface-modified sodium bicarbonate, stable across packing forms, "not only desiccant tubes": 15–25% of a tablet or sachet formula as the effervescent system, or 1–4% for sparkle in a chewable or fast-melt; the concept is a fizzy electrolyte and energy stick for sport (slide 8) (slide 10) [00:01].
- Fast-melt: Advantol 300 with Effer-Soda 12 disintegrates in under 10 seconds, 20–40% faster than typical direct-compression competitors, and uses standard packaging, not lyophilised blisters (slide 14) (slide 15). It is aimed at children and older people who cannot or will not swallow [00:01].
- Nordic Biotic: full exclusivity by territory at strain level; genomics, then metabolomics (630 molecules, "the postbiotics actually"), then functional testing, then human studies [09:25].
- Mental Balance: eight-week study, 227 students started, 219 completed, "one of the largest study in stress in probiotics in the last decade"; results received last week; he said statistical efficacy was proved in sleep quality and stress [09:25].
- Nordic Biotic has a GLP-1 companion product with its Swiss sister company Azento [09:25].

## For Vita Actives
