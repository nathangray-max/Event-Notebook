# From hype to healthspan: Building the future of longevity nutrition

## Summary
Stephen Daniells of NutraIngredients chaired two retailers, a brand and a marketplace: Jessica Pasco of Holland & Barrett, Rachel Kreider of GNC, Federico Luna of Timeline and Jennifer Wang of Alibaba's Taobao Tmall. His question: is longevity a real category, or an umbrella for outcomes consumers already understand?

They agreed that shoppers pay for benefits they can feel or see. Prevention alone is hard to sell, so proof and a tangible result, such as stronger muscles or better skin, matter most. They also agreed that GLP-1 medicines move people into a proactive mindset, and that creatine and muscle are a way into healthy ageing.

They differed on the category itself. Luna said longevity is absolutely a category, defined by the hallmarks of ageing and four pillars, and that bundling a generic vitamin C into it does consumers a disservice. Pasco said Holland & Barrett's new in-store longevity category is small but growing fast, driven mainly by NAD, while most proactive shopping is not badged longevity. Kreider said GNC treats healthy ageing as a demand space and links new ingredients to condition-specific products. Wang said nobody in China searches for longevity online: men search for sexual vitality and women for sagging skin, and premium shoppers ask for patents and clinical studies.

## Key takeaways
- Holland & Barrett created a longevity category in store over the last six months; it is "still quite small" but has seen "really significant growth", driven primarily by NAD, with spermidine less popular [02:58] [03:47].
- GNC defines healthy ageing as a target demand space and is extending its condition-specific preventive nutrition brand into standalone longevity ingredients such as NAD+; Kreider said consumers need to connect it to a benefit they can feel [08:44].
- Online in China, longevity is not a search term: the trigger for men is sexual vitality and for women sagging skin, followed by joints, sleep and metabolism [06:09].
- GLP-1 as a gateway: Pasco sees customers entering the category because they start a GLP-1 journey and staying after they stop the medication [14:52]; Kreider sees an opportunity for products that protect muscle on that journey [15:59].
- Creatine is crossing over: Kreider said people engage with healthy ageing through creatine and active nutrition, and Pasco said creatine is "moving from a more of a sports focus into this longevity space" [17:19] [19:15].
- Proof decides the role of an ingredient: Kreider wants hero ingredients with clinical substantiation and a tangible benefit, with buzzier ingredients as background [31:12]; Wang said Chinese shoppers ask for a branded ingredient, a patent and a clinical study, and pay the premium [22:45].
