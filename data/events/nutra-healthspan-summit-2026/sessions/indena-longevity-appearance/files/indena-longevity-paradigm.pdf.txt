CUTOMETER® METHOD
The measurement of skin elasticity is based on the suction method using
a negative pressure mechanically deforming the skin, which is drawn into
the aperture of a probe for 2 seconds and after a defined time (2 seconds)
released again. The light intensity in the probe varies due to the
penetration depth of the skin.
R2 parameter (gross elasticity or overall elasticity) represents the
ability of the skin to return to its basal state; an increase of R2
parameter indicates an improvement of skin elasticity .
