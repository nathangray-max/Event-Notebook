## Summary

Ewa Hudson presents insights on the generational shift in longevity aspirations and the evolving role of the healthspan industry.

## Consumer Trends
**Generational Shifts**
* Younger populations view healthy aging as a preventative opportunity rather than a reactive one. 
* While 39% of those aged 65+ believe genetics play a major role in aging, only 21% of younger respondents share this view.
* Interest in supplements is higher among younger cohorts; 20% of the 25–34 age group believe in their efficacy compared to only 12% of those over 65.
* Aging well is increasingly defined by vitality and the ability to reinvent oneself, exemplified by rising numbers of entrepreneurs over age 50 and extreme athletes.

**Demographic Needs**
* **Energy and Stamina**: Identified as the top health concern for both men and women across all age brackets.
* **Gender Specifics**: Women prioritize mobility, joint health, and bone health, while men focus heavily on exercise and healthy eating as they age.
* **Age-Specific Drivers**: For women in their late 30s, sleep is a primary concern, whereas weight management and muscle strength become more critical ten years later.

## Industry Evolution
**Business Models**
* Companies are transitioning from product-focused entities to purpose-driven service platforms. Examples include **Niagara**, which moved into IV injections and pharma-adjacent spaces, and **Life Extension**, which offers biological age blood tests to allow consumers to track supplement efficacy through data.
* **Novos** is repositioning itself from a supplement brand to a "health intelligence company."

**Challenges and Opportunities**
* **The Trust Gap**: The primary hesitation for consumers regarding longevity supplements is "not enough proof," signaling a need for increased scientific validation.
* **Technological Integration**: AI tools like **ChatGPT** are becoming central to how consumers seek personalized advice, necessitating that brands provide high-quality scientific data to feed these models.
* **GLP-1 Impact**: The rise of **GLP-1** treatments is shifting consumer behaviors toward healthier food choices, increased interest in longevity, and decreased alcohol consumption.

How can companies better bridge the trust gap through applied science? How will the rise of AI-driven personal planning reshape supplement marketing?
