BSc at University of Glasgow and PhD at Durham University. Following research posts in

What is the ISI?
The Insomnia Severity Index is a validated questionnaire
assessing the severity of insomnia symptoms and their
daytime impact. Lower scores indicate milder
symptoms. Reported as symptom severity within the
NMN group during supplementation, not as a diagnosis
or a treatment claim for insomnia.
BETWEEN-GROUP COMPARISON
The ISI score in the NMN group was significantly lower than in
the placebo group (p < 0.01) at Day 56.

What is deep sleep?
Deep sleep is part of non-rapid eye movement (NREM)
sleep and an important component of normal sleep
architecture. A longer duration suggests more
restorative sleep.
Clinical interpretation
The observed increase provides an additional sleep-related
finding alongside the change in overall sleep quality. It is
described as a change within the NMN group.

Baseline comparison after intervention was statistically significant (p < 0.05) within the NMN group.
