# Fairfield Nutech: a two to three year exit plan

## Summary
On the first day of FiE, Jordan, a friend of Nathan's who runs a company dictated as "Fairfield Nutech", told him they are looking at a two to three year exit plan. Nathan describes it as a specialist protein house with interesting technologies.

The company is most likely Fayrefield Nutratek, a sports-protein business in Crewe set up in June 2025. Its director Jordan Donohue has owned 20% from the start, and the name, the first name and the protein technology all match, though nothing public calls him chief executive. The rest is owned by Fayrefield's UK dairy-trading group and its Danish sister company, 37.5% each, with 5% held by a second director.

Nutratek sells whey, milk-protein and casein ingredients, including clear whey and hydrolysates, application systems for drinks, yoghurt, powders and bars, and a protein coffee. Since May 2026 it has been the exclusive UK and Western Europe distributor of Jenerise creatine, which competes with CreaWin.

It has filed no accounts yet. The UK parent group turned over £254.8 million in 2024. A Danish private equity firm took 60% of the Danish parent in late 2025, so any sale needs both parents to agree. No public sign of a sale was found.
