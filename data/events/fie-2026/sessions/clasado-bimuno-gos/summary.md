# Clasado (Bimuno GOS): possible sale

## Summary
On the first day of FiE, Clasado's chief executive told Nathan the company would look for a sale price of probably 10 to 12 million. Nathan knows him well. Clasado sells about 3 million a year, is not yet profitable and hopes to be within two years. Nathan sees room for its one ingredient, Bimuno GOS, to reach 8 to 10 million in sales.

Clasado is a Reading company owned through Jersey by Liechtenstein trusts. Per Rehné has been chief executive since 2019, and the dictated "CEO pair" is most likely "CEO Per". It sells one ingredient, Bimuno, a prebiotic made from milk sugar, protected by patents and backed by more than 25 clinical trials, two of them in athletes. It has no authorised health claim in the EU but has claims in Australia, Colombia and other markets.

Its accounts show a loss every year. Turnover was £5.1 million in 2024, but that included the Bimuno consumer business, sold to ELEAT at the start of 2025, so the ingredient business alone fits the 3 million Nathan heard. The company owes its Jersey parent £41.8 million, and its auditors have twice flagged doubt over its ability to continue without that support. In October 2026 it appointed ACI Group as its UK and Ireland distributor.

## Key takeaways
- Price talk from the chief executive: "they would be looking for 10 to 12,000,000 probably as a sale price" (notes).
- Size and profit as Nathan heard them: about 3 million in sales, "not at all profitable yet but hope to be in the next two years" (notes).
- Nathan's view of the upside: "they could easily be doing 8 to 10 million" (notes).
- The filed accounts show losses of £2.5 to £4.0 million a year from 2021 to 2024, a £41.8 million interest-free loan from the Jersey parent repayable on demand, and net liabilities of £38.6 million at the end of 2025. My reading: what happens to that loan decides what a buyer really pays.
- My reading: the business is now ingredient-only. The consumer Bimuno brand went to ELEAT on 1 Jan 2025, so the 2024 turnover of £5.1 million overstates today's ingredient sales.
- No authorised EU health claim: EFSA found no cause and effect for the gut-discomfort claim. Claims exist in Australia (29 relationships) and Colombia (11).

## For Vita Actives
- Relevance high: the most concrete of the three FiE leads, with a price named by the chief executive, whom Nathan knows well (notes).
- My reading: a sale price of 10 to 12 million is about two to four times the sales Nathan heard, before the parent loan is settled.
- My reading: Bimuno fits our sports and healthy-ageing work. It has athlete trials from Nottingham Trent (2016 and 2026; the 2026 trial reported less gut-barrier damage at 2.75 g a day for six weeks, per Clasado's release) and trials in people aged 52 to 65.
- My reading: without an EU claim, selling it in Europe rests on the studies, not on a claim on pack.
- My reading: short of buying, Clasado is now a business-to-business supplier building distribution; ACI Group took the UK and Ireland in October 2026, so Vita Actives could look at other territories or be a competitor for that role.

## Research
Desk research on public sources, 17 Nov 2026. Nothing Nathan was told went into a search.
- Identity: Clasado Limited, Companies House 05268862, incorporated 25 Oct 2004, Reading; from University of Reading research. US subsidiary Clasado, Inc. (Delaware).
- Ownership: wholly owned by Clasado Biosciences Limited (Jersey). Ultimate controlling party Lexus Trust (Vaduz, Liechtenstein), "financed by Plexus Trust". Four people with significant control, each controlling a trust's trustees, resident in Liechtenstein or Switzerland; two more ceased on 6 May 2026. Ownership before December 2018 was not found; no funding rounds are recorded.
- Chief executive: Per Rehné, formerly commercial director of OptiBiotix; director from 4 Mar 2019, reported as CEO on 13 Mar 2019. Graham Waters was CEO in 2016.
- Product: Bimuno GOS, made from lactose with enzymes from *B. bifidum* NCIMB 41171, as powder or syrup; patents US 7883874 and CA 2520043. The company states GRAS status, EU and UK novel foods compliance, Halal and Kosher, and "100+ claims available for select markets". More than 130 publications and 25+ clinical trials (company figures).
- Accounts (Clasado Limited, audited): turnover £3,108,014 and loss £2,511,144 (2021); £2,616,421 and £2,833,954 (2022); £3,322,615 and £3,969,771 (2023); £5,130,284 and £2,565,605 (2024). 2024 gross profit £3,183,553. 2025 filed as small-company accounts with no profit and loss account: net liabilities £38,572,000, parent loan £41,766,000, 23 staff. Going-concern material uncertainty flagged in the 2022 and 2024 accounts.
- Consumer business: sold on 1 Jan 2025 to H2Nutrition Ltd, announced in April 2025 as a sale to ELEAT (products, customers and a UK licence). Inference: H2Nutrition is ELEAT's company, as ELEAT's co-founder is its director.
- Manufacturing: no plant named; plant at cost totals £200,660, fully depreciated. Inference: production is outsourced.
- Distribution: ACI Group, UK and Ireland (21 Oct 2026); Pharmanutra, Italy; Prokopton, France; Globofarm, India; Phytonet, 14 markets. Bimuno Pet launched October 2024.
- Changes: auditor changed November 2024; the sister research company was folded in and dissolved on 22 Sep 2026; new VP Sales (October 2025) and Asia-Pacific director (April 2026). No public sale process or advisers.
- Competitors: FrieslandCampina GOS (16 Australian claim relationships). Inference: Ingredion, Yakult/Nissin GOS, and inulin and FOS suppliers (Beneo, Sensus).
