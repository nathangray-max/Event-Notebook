# AIDP: possible sale within two years

## Summary
On the first day of FiE, Nathan heard that AIDP will be up for sale within the next two years. He called it a massive company with over 100 million in US revenue: probably too big for Vita Actives alone, but a fantastic acquisition for us or someone else. His note does not say who told him.

AIDP is a privately held US ingredient company in City of Industry, California, founded in 1996 by Edward Lee. Lee died unexpectedly; his death was announced in March 2026. Jennifer Gu, his partner and head of research since the start, is now chief executive. A founder's sudden death is a common reason for a sale.

Its main owned asset is Magtein, magnesium L-threonate, with US GRAS status and EU and UK novel food authorisations that carry five years of data protection. Since 2024 a separate company, ThreoTech, has run Magtein, so a buyer of AIDP alone might not get it. AIDP also owns prebiotic GOS technology bought from France's Olygose in 2020, and distributes about 40 partner brands, among them collagen, plant protein and botanicals.

No accounts are public. AIDP says it has more than 60 staff, and public revenue estimates run from 6.9 million to 100 million dollars, below Nathan's figure. It has no European office.

- My reading: the founder's death, announced in March 2026, is a likely trigger for a sale; no adviser or sale process is public.
- My reading: Magtein is the prize, and its ownership is split. AIDP holds the EU data rights, ThreoTech (separate since 2024) runs the brand, the patent licence and its staff.

## Research
Desk research on public sources, 17 Nov 2026. Nothing Nathan was told went into a search.
- Identity: AIDP, Inc. ("Advanced Ingredients for Dietary Products"), 19535 East Walnut Drive South, City of Industry, CA; founded 1996 by Edward Lee, PhD; started in commodity trading and moved to clinically backed branded ingredients in the mid-2000s.
- Leadership: Lee's death was announced on 2 to 3 March 2026 and described as unexpected. Jennifer Gu, PhD, his "lifelong partner" and head of R&D since the start, became CEO of AIDP and ThreoTech and took his board seats. President: Mark Thurston. Ownership is not stated anywhere public.
- Magtein (magnesium L-threonate): US GRAS since 2012; EU novel food from 7 Nov 2024 with five years of data exclusivity (Regulation 2024/2694), held by AIDP with ThreoTech as licensee; UK novel food authorised March 2026 with five years of data protection; in "over 400 products"; a 33% five-year US growth rate and sold through Walmart, Target and Costco (ThreoTech, April 2025). Patents trace to Magceutics (an MIT and Tsinghua discovery). Two Vitafoods 2026 awards.
- ThreoTech, LLC: the "standalone" Magtein company, created April 2024 by one account and spun off in 2025 by another; its ownership is not disclosed.
- Prebiotics: bought Olygose SAS's core technology and IP in October 2020 (AlphaGOS, pea GOS, a soluble pea protein process; 16 patents claimed); also NeoGOS and NuSweet.
- Partner brands on its site: collagen (Naticol, Keragen-IV, Vollagen, KoAct, kollaGen II-xs), plant protein (RisaPro, Peasipro), botanicals (BacoMind, Turmacin, GutGard, LongJaX, Saffr'Activ, Liboost), VegD3, K2VITAL, Livaux, Actazin, PureMune and Cognilipid.
- Size: no filed accounts; "more than 60 team members worldwide" (March 2026); Owler estimates 25 to 100 million dollars revenue, ZoomInfo 6.9 million dollars. No funding rounds, investment, sale process or advisers found.
- Europe: no AIDP office found; it exhibited Magtein at Fi Europe 2025 and Vitafoods.
