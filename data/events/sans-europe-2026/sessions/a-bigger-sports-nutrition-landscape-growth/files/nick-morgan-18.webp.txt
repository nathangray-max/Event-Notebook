HOW BRANDS EXPLAIN THEIR ELECTROLYTES (Approach: Explanation and brand example)
Number & completeness: SiS highlights five essential electrolytes. Number reassures, and the premise of "comprehensive" seems intuitive.
Sweat-loss replacement: Veloforte links minerals to sweat losses, giving electrolytes a clear purpose and reason to choose.
Dose & strength: LMNT promotes a meaningful dose. Explain who it suits, rather than implying that more means better.
Blend, ratio & science: Bulk promotes optimum ratios, communicating why the chosen blend is right for the need.
Personal relevance: Precision matches strengths to sweat losses, helping consumers choose the right product for their needs.
Natural sources & wider benefits: Hunter & Gather uses natural minerals and daily balance, adding reasons to buy beyond the blend.
