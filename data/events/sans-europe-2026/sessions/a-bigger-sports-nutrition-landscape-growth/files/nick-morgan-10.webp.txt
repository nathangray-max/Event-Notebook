Plant protein vs just protein
Rocka range. Pro Protein: "Vegan Performance Protein". Higher protein dose (24g) + a slightly higher calorie content (122kcals) to achieve a "smooth, creamy, indulgent taste". 83% plant protein blend [fava bean & pea]. EUR 37.99 per 700g tub [EUR 1.65 per serve].
Natural Protein: "Naturally-flavoured & a monk-fruit sweetened powder". A slightly lower protein dose (21g) & reduced calorie content (112kcals). 86.6% plant protein blend [soy protein isolate, fava bean, almond & pea protein]. EUR 29.99 per 600g tub [EUR 1.50 per serve].
Protein Shake: "The tastiest vegan protein powder". Reflects a similar profile to the Natural formulation yet it doubles down on "shake" terminology. 84% plant protein blend [soy, pea, almond & fava bean protein]. EUR 37.99 per 900g tub [EUR 1.27 per serve].
