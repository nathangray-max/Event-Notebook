Establishing pillars of innovation (full slide, six points)
01 Whey choice risks confusing consumers: As new whey formats multiply, shoppers need clearer cues on which product is for who and when to use it.
02 Communications react more than they solve: The category leans on comparisons; the opportunity is to lead with propositions that solve a consumer problem.
03 Hybrid powders are gaining consistency: After a slow start, launches increasingly combine dairy and plant protein in overt hybrid blends.
04 Caseinate is the new milkshake: More brands are quietly shifting towards caseinate as a viable protein source instead of milk protein.
05 Plant protein still needs a taste breakthrough: Brands are revisiting plant protein, but launch pace suggests limited confidence that taste and texture are solved.
06 The next cafe collaboration race is on: Myprotein >< Nero launch signals the race for the next out-of-home cafe collaboration.
