Challenging economics
European Whey Protein Prices (EUR/kg), Jan 2023 - Jul 2026. Lines: WPI, WPC80, WPC35.
Chart: WPI from about 11 EUR/kg in Jan 2023 to 30.5 EUR/kg; WPC80 from about 7 EUR/kg to 27.6 EUR/kg; WPC35 from about 3 to 4.3 EUR/kg. Both WPI and WPC80 rise steeply in 2026.
Callout "Historic acceleration": Demand continues to exceed available WPI and WPC80 production capacity, resulting in the strongest period of price inflation on record.
# WPC 80 and WPI 90 have increased approximately fourfold from 2023, with the sharpest acceleration occurring during H1 2026.
Source: Vesper Price Index (EU), EXW, EUR/kg.
