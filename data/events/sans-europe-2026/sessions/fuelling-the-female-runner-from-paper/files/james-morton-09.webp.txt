Conclusion slide: "Female trained runners can oxidise exogenous CHO at comparable rates to male runners. Furthermore, in both sexes, 120 g/h maintains whole body CHO oxidation, CHO dependency and reduces the oxygen cost of running (i.e. improves running economy) compared with traditional CHO guidelines of 60-90 g/h. However, individual variations in rates of exogenous CHO oxidation and gastrointestinal symptoms warrant CHO personalisation strategies."
