Closing statement: "The biggest barrier to fuelling the female runner is not likely a biological limitation i.e. their gut or their muscles. Rather, it is lack of knowledge and awareness of the guidelines (i.e. educational) and the associated physiological and performance benefits, especially when considering a world where under-fuelling has been prevalent. Fuelling is less about hormones and tailoring to the menstrual cycle, put simply, it is about fuelling for the work required." Background: crates of SiS Run Fuel gels at an After Dark event.
