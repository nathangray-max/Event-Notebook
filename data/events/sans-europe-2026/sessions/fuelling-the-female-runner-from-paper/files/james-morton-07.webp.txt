Males and females can oxidise exogenous CHO in a dose dependent manner, where 120 > 90 > 60 g.
Peak exogenous CHO oxidation (g/min), individual points with means. MALES: 60 g/h about 1.0; 90 g/h about 1.5 (*); 120 g/h about 1.8 (**). FEMALES: 60 g/h about 1.0; 90 g/h about 1.45 (*); 120 g/h about 1.75 (**). About 7 to 8 participants per group.
Males: Ravikanti et al. (2025). J App Physiol, 139, 1581-1595. Females: Ravikanti et al. (2026). Under revision.
