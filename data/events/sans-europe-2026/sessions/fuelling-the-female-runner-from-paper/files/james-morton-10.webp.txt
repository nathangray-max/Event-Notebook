Fuelling framework (partial view, right side): CHO oxidation (g/min) against exercise duration (hours), by intensity zone. Up to 1 hour: HIT training 30-60 g single source (mouth rinse, drinks, gels); 0-30 g single source; 0 g in recovery. 1 to 2 hours: half marathon 90 g dual source; soccer 60-90 g single-dual source; 30-60 g single source (drinks, gels, chews). 2 to 3 hours: marathon 120 g dual source; 90-120 g dual source; 60-90 g single-dual source (drinks, gels, chews, bars).
