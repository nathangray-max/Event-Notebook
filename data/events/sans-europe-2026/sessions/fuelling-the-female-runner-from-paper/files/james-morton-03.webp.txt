120 g/h maintains whole body CHO oxidation to a greater extent than traditional sport nutrition guidelines of 90 g/h and 60 g/h.
MALES. Chart: CHO oxidation (g/min) over 120 min for 60, 90 and 120 g/h. 120 g/h rises to about 3.2 g/min at 45 min and stays about 3.0 until 105 min, falling to about 2.2 at 120 min; 90 g/h about 2.5 to 2.6 then falling to about 1.6; 60 g/h about 2.1 to 2.2 then falling to about 1.2.
Ravikanti et al. (2025). J App Physiol, 139, 1581-1595.
