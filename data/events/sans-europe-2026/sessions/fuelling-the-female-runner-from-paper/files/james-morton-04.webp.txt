With 60 g/h, the "crossover" from carbohydrate to fat dependency occurs after approximately 60 minutes of running.
MALES and FEMALES: energy expenditure (kJ/min) from CHO and from fat over 120 min. In both sexes CHO falls and fat rises, crossing at about 60 to 75 min (males: CHO from about 40 to about 28, fat from about 33 to about 50; females: CHO from about 26 to 33 down to about 14, fat from about 26 to about 41).
Males: Ravikanti et al. (2025). J App Physiol, 139, 1581-1595. Females: Ravikanti et al. (2026). Under revision.
