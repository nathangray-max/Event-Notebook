With 120 g/h, the "crossover" from carbohydrate to fat dependency is completely prevented in both sexes.
MALES: CHO about 46 kJ/min at 15 min, about 58 to 60 from 30 to 90 min, about 44 at 120 min; fat about 22 to 32 throughout. FEMALES: CHO about 37 at 15 min, about 44 to 47 from 30 to 75 min, about 33 at 120 min; fat about 11 to 18 throughout.
Males: Ravikanti et al. (2025). J App Physiol, 139, 1581-1595. Females: Ravikanti et al. (2026). Under revision.
