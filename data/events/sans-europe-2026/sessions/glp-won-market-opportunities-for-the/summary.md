# GLP-Won - Market Opportunities for the Active Nutrition Sector

## Summary
Morgan Griffiths of Lumina Intelligence argued that GLP-1, bad news for food and drink brands, is an opportunity for active nutrition [00:08]. He said 6.1% of the UK population currently take GLP-1s, 5.7% have taken them and 15.1% are considering them, about 18.4 million people, with similar current use in Italy, France, Germany and Spain [00:08]. Current users are mostly female, Gen X or Millennial and ABC1, already health-minded and willing to pay more; two thirds expect to spend the same or more on food and drink, and near 60% are likely to increase their supplement use [00:08]. He pointed to three opportunity areas: side effects, nutrition gaps such as protein and muscle loss, and lapsed users, who he said are four times more likely to regain weight according to a British Medical Association study [00:08]. Huel, Cambridge Nutrition, Portion IQ and Boots are already moving, and Lumina forecasts UK current use at around 11% by 2031 [00:08].

## Key takeaways
- UK use now: 6.1% currently taking GLP-1s, 5.7% previously, 15.1% considering; he said the considering figure jumped "by about two thirds, a third to a half" when the pill was introduced [00:08].
- Together that is about 18.4 million people: everyone in the UK born since 2000, the UK's Netflix subscribers, or the 12th largest country in Europe [00:08].
- A summer study found similar current use in Italy, France, Germany and Spain; the US is further ahead [00:08].
- Current users (6.1%) outnumber vegans and vegetarians combined (5.7%), yet food and drink teams invest more in vegan innovation [00:08].
- Current users are not the obesity population the drugs were designed for: 56% female, 63% Gen X or Millennial, 63% ABC1 [00:08].
- They are health-minded: over 55% already make healthy lifestyle choices, 62% look for food with added benefits and 74% are happy to pay more for quality [00:08].
- Spend: the most common answer was no change, the net was an increase, and two thirds expect to spend the same or more on food and drink; 90 to 95% in the UK buy the drugs privately, at "one to 300 pounds a month" [00:08].
- Supplements: near 60% are likely to increase supplement use, near 90% with no change included; the list held no surprises, with creatine "maybe slightly higher" than expected, and he saw "a little bit of white space" for active nutrition [00:08].
- Opportunity areas: lapsed users (30% worry about putting weight back on), side effects, reduced food enjoyment and access to nutrition (muscle loss concerns, difficulty consuming enough protein and nutrients), with a malnutrition risk for people who eat less without care [00:08].
- Lapsed users: around 80% think they will keep the weight off (95% including neutral), but he cited a British Medical Association study showing people are four times more likely to regain weight lost with GLP-1 than with traditional methods [00:08].
- Why: GLP-1 changes behaviour without the user changing behaviour; when "you turn the tap off", the weight comes back unless habits changed [00:08].
- Market moves: Huel's lighter version, Cambridge Nutrition and Portion IQ ranges, and Boots' GLP-1 bays and shelf clips; putting GLP-1 on pack is "a kind of a health claim", so retailer support matters [00:08].
- Forecast: Lumina's data has UK current users growing to around 11% by 2031 [00:08].

## For Vita Actives
