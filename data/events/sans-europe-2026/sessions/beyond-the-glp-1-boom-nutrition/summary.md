# Beyond the GLP-1 Boom: Nutrition, Innovation and the Future of Weight Management

## Summary
The day's last panel asked what GLP-1 means for sports and active nutrition beyond the boom. It brought together Nick Morgan of Nutrition Integrated, Brett Hamer, product director at Myprotein, and Ed Tooley, a performance nutritionist.

They agreed that GLP-1 is speeding up trends that already existed rather than creating new ones, and that few brands put GLP-1 on pack: Myprotein sells need-state bundles, and retailer bays such as Boots' let brands be present without the wording. Brett said clear protein, and clear protein with collagen, is selling very well to this consumer, while plant protein has not taken off; Nick put that down to taste. Nick called creatine almost a tier one ingredient, now bought by women, older consumers and people managing their weight.

They differed in where they looked. Ed's concern was nutrition: people who simply eat less risk missing protein, carbohydrate and micronutrients, so he puts protein first. Brett and Nick looked at products: both would launch protein with fibre, and Nick named low-volume clear protein with fibre and products for people coming off the drugs as the gaps. For the next disruptor, Brett named people coming off the drugs, Ed education between on and off, and Nick AI.

The recording ends with the host's close of the day.

- Brett: clear protein is "performing extremely well" in retail, and clear protein plus collagen is "just a sweet spot for this this consumer", with retail launches coming [35:09], [43:51].

- Ed: people who simply eat less risk missing key nutrients, with a risk of RED-S and deficiencies; for low appetite he puts protein first, then carbohydrate, then micronutrients [32:49], [34:13].
