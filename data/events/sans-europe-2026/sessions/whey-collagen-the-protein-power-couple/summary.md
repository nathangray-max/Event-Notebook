# Whey & Collagen: The Protein Power Couple

## Summary
Tess Plaquet, technical sport manager at Rousselot, argued that brands should stop asking when whey prices will fall and work around them by blending whey with collagen.

She began with the market. Whey launches keep growing, and she said the forces behind whey prices, from GLP-1 users and China to whey being a cheese by-product and rising energy and labour costs, mean high prices are here to stay. Brands can raise prices, shrink packs or reformulate; she called smarter formulation with protein blends the most elegant answer. Launches combining whey and collagen are growing at almost 17.5% a year, and collagen comes from four animal sources with far steadier prices.

She then made the case that the two proteins complement rather than compete: whey builds and repairs muscle, while Rousselot's Peptan collagen peptides support tendons, joints and bone. Her evidence was Rousselot's own studies. The central one gave 22 men either 45 g of whey or 25 g of whey plus 20 g of Peptan for three weeks of training, and recovery and performance were similar in both groups. Studies of Peptan on its own reported less muscle soreness, fewer blood endotoxins after a run, better knee comfort and better sleep.

The recording ends as she invites the room to Rousselot's roundtables.

## Key takeaways
- Brands have three responses to whey costs: raise the price, shrinkflation, or smarter formulation with protein blends, which she called the most elegant and a way to stand out on shelf [00:10].
- Launches combining whey and collagen are growing at almost 17.5 percent a year; a 10 g collagen plus 10 g whey product was her example [00:10].
- Collagen supply is spread across bovine, porcine, chicken and fish, and its historical prices fluctuate far less than whey's [00:10].
- Whey and collagen are "no competitors": whey for muscle, Peptan (rich in hydroxyproline, glycine and proline) for ligaments, tendons, joints and bone [00:10].
- In 22 men over three weeks of training, 25 g whey plus 20 g Peptan gave the same recovery and performance as 45 g whey [00:10].
- 20 g Peptan with vitamin C for seven days gave 28% less muscle soreness and 13% faster muscle recovery than placebo after 150 jumps [00:10].

## Detail
- What she said about whey: launches of whey food and beverage supplements have grown steadily for two years at seven and a half percent a year; the biggest price rise was in 24 to 25, "with a growth rate of 8.7"; and "in 2023 the price of a ton of whey protein concentrate of 80% was 5.7 k, whereas in 2026 it's almost 32 k. So really, in just three years' time, it's almost a sixfold" [00:10].
- Her drivers, which she said mean "those prices will be here to stay": demand from active lifestyle consumers, GLP-1 users advised to take extra protein, and China; whey is a by-product of cheese and cannot be scaled easily; gas, electricity, labour, filtration costs, tariffs, geopolitics and climate push the same way [00:10].
- Whey is a complete protein rich in BCAAs, for muscle building and repair. Peptan is hydrolysed collagen, rich in hydroxyproline (unique to animal-derived collagen and, she said, linked to holistic health benefits), glycine and proline [00:10].
- The head-to-head: 22 men, 45 g whey against 25 g whey plus 20 g Peptan, three weeks of training, measured on jump height and creatine kinase; no difference between groups at the end [00:10].
- Soreness: 24 men, 20 g Peptan with vitamin C or placebo for seven days, then 150 jumps [00:10].
- Endotoxins: 10 g Peptan for seven days, then 45 minutes before a 70-minute run; endotoxins were the same as without exercise, while on placebo they rose. Her hedge: "...it's really an indication that taking in peptan could lessen the amount of endotoxins in your blood and could reduce your digestive discomfort" [00:10].
- Knees: women with mild knee discomfort, 8 g a day for six months; significant improvement in discomfort and function at three months, and further at six [00:10].
- Sleep: 15 g a day, polysomnography on night seven; awakenings fell from around 30 to 22, subjective sleep improved, and the Peptan group scored 100% on a Stroop test against a weaker placebo group [00:10].
- Rousselot offers roundtables on application advantages and help incorporating collagen [00:10].
