Where the evidence runs thin
FEMALE ATHLETES
Most bone-marker studies are in men; menstrual
status adds complexity.
ADOLESCENTS
Peak bone mass is being built, yet direct data are
scarce.
OUTCOMES
We measure markers over hours and weeks, not
fractures over years.
DOSE AND TIMING
How much carbohydrate, and when, is needed to
protect bone isn't yet defined.
