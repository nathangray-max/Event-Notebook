Why are we talking about it? (slide 2)
Record highs this summer (WPC80 up roughly 6x since 2020, WPI near EUR 32/kg), now correcting.
Chart: EU price (EUR/kg), WPC80 and WPI, 2020 to 2026. WPC80 from about EUR 5 in 2020, peak about EUR 12 in 2022, trough about EUR 5 in 2023, then a steep rise to about EUR 26-27 in mid 2026, correcting to about EUR 18 at the last point. WPI from about EUR 8 in 2020, peak about EUR 18 in 2022, trough about EUR 7 in 2023, rising to about EUR 31 in mid 2026, correcting to about EUR 27.
Source: Vesper VPI, EU monthly prices (EUR/kg), Dec 2019 to Oct 2026.
