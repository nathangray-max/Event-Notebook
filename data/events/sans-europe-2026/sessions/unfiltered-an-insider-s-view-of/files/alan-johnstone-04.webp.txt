How whey protein is made, and why there's so little of it (slide 4)
The production journey: from raw milk to refined whey protein. Raw milk (~3.4% protein) -> coagulation (rennet) -> Cheese (the primary product) -> whey drained off -> Liquid whey (co-product of cheese) -> ultrafiltration + drying -> WPC (35-80% protein) -> microfiltration / ion exchange -> WPI (>=90% protein) -> acidify or hydrolyse -> Clear / Hydrolysed WPI (>=90%, refined).
What's in milk (by weight): Water 87.3%; Lactose 4.7%; Fat 3.9%; Casein 2.8%; Minerals 0.7%; Whey 0.6% (whey protein).
Other Whey Derivatives. Beyond protein, there are other options and by-products for producers: Sweet whey powder (~12% protein); Whey permeate (lactose + minerals); Lactose (purified milk sugar); Demineralised whey (D70 / D90, infant nutrition); Procream / WFC (whey cream / fat).
Source: typical cow's-milk composition & dairy processing, indicative industry figures.
