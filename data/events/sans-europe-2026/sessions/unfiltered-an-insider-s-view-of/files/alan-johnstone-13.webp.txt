Volatility (slide 8, first build)
There are many causes and effects of volatility - not just supply and demand.
Chart: EU price (EUR/kg), WPC80 and WPI, Dec 2019 to mid 2024, with three notes. 2021: Logistics constraints. Lead times grow. Stocks need to rise. 2022 peak: Logistics free up. Lead times fall. Buyers destock. 2023 trough: Falling prices. Buyers wait as long as possible. Stock levels too low.
Source: Vesper VPI, EU monthly prices (EUR/kg), Dec 2019 to mid 2024.
