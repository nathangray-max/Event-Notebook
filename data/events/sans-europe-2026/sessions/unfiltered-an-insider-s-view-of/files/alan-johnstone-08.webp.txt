Five years of supply growth: WPC80 and WPI (slide 6, first build)
Global production, 2020 vs 2025. Pie areas drawn to scale.
2020: WPC80 334,000 t; WPI 80,494 t; total 414,494 t. 2025: WPC80 360,000 t; WPI 161,800 t; total 521,800 t. +26%.
+1.5% WPC80 CAGR (5-yr); +15.0% WPI CAGR (5-yr); +4.7% Total CAGR (5-yr).
WPC80 is getting investment, but it is also losing whey to WPI so looks flat. Both are taking whey from lower protein derivatives.
