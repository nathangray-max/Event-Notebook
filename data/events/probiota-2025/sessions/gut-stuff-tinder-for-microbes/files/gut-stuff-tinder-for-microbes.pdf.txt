--- page 11 ---
probiotic problems
Customers expect immediate results
The British Dietetic Association recommends taking probiotics for at least four
weeks to give them the opportunity to work as it takes time for the probiotic
bacteria being introduced to the gut to become established and start influencing
neural and chemical signaling. Yet the new PrecisionBiotics® research found many
consumers look for almost immediate results, with 19% expecting changes within a
day and 18% in a week.
Customers aren’t tracking how the product is improving symptoms or lifestyle
factors on a day to day basis so they have little motivation to keep taking it
Customers forget to take it everyday
Companies don’t have enough meaningful data on their customer to know exactly
who they are and if the product is actually helping them
Consumers don’t know which probiotic to take and who they can trust
Customers don’t understand how to get the most out of their probiotic (the full gut
health 360 picture)
