THE SCIENCE: GUT-MUSCLE AXIS
The bidirectional GUT-Muscle Axis
leads the adaptation to training and
affects maximal performance.
How?
Microbial metabolites, in response to
stress induced by exercise, support
muscle metabolism and physiology
through mechanisms of control of
inflammation and oxidative stress,
higher glucose and fat oxidation,
mitochondrial activity and biogenesis,
muscle contraction and growth etc...
Irisin and IL-6, from contracting muscle,
stimulate the intestinal release of
ghrelin, leptin and GLP-1, affecting the
appetite, the pain and inflammatory
response at the CNS level
Liu et al., 2023 Jul:163:114837. DOI: 10.1016/j.biopha.2023.114837
