# Alba Health (Probiota Pioneers 2025)

## Summary
Nora Cavani, co-founder of Alba Health, pitched as one of NutraIngredients' Probiota Pioneers for 2025. Alba Health, based in Sweden, sells a gut microbiome test for children aged nought to two, with a diet plan and probiotic recommendations.

She started the company after her own eczema. Her argument is that how microbes settle in a baby's gut in the first years is linked to later asthma, obesity, eczema and allergies, and to everyday symptoms such as colic and constipation that have no good answer today. Testing early could spot imbalances and correct them with diet, lifestyle and possibly probiotics.

Her edge is data: a partnership with a University of Helsinki study that followed 1,000 children for over six years, and its own Swedish cohort of 300 children. Customers report their children's eczema or constipation easing, though no trial was presented.

In the panel she said her biggest hurdle is consumer education. Alba Health sells no probiotics of its own and recommends other companies' products with clinical evidence; she said most customers buy the one recommended. Given money for one thing, she would spend it on marketing.

## Key takeaways
- The product: "a gut microbiome test specifically developed for children, zero to two years old", used to detect imbalances early, restore them with diet and possibly probiotics, and track development [14:42].
- Evidence claimed is the partner cohort: 1,000 children followed for over six years, over 10,000 samples, over 50,000 data points on diet and health; she said a 2024 paper showed an early-life test predicts later allergies, coughs, colds and crying [14:42].
- Results so far are customer reports: "a lot of our users and customers tell us that their children's eczema or constipation significantly reduced" [14:42].
- Alba Health recommends existing probiotics from other companies and takes no profit on them; "most of our customers buy the probiotic we recommend" [49:19].
- Trust comes from a scientific advisory board with doctors from each market, and simple messages through social media and podcasts [47:04].
- 90% of customers opt into research [14:42] [51:58].

## For Vita Actives
- Relevance low: a test for infants, outside our sports and healthy-ageing markets and our range.
- My reading: an independent recommender that sells nothing itself can drive which branded probiotic a parent buys. That is a channel worth knowing for any partner with a clinically backed strain.
- My reading: the panel's shared lesson, that education is the hurdle and that a neutral expert voice beats ads, applies to how we pitch evidence-based ingredients to brands.

## Detail
- Figures she gave: one in three people in the US has an allergic disease and almost two in three a chronic disease; the UN puts chronic diseases' cost at $47 trillion by 2030; 40% of US children have at least one chronic disease; over 50% of babies have unresolved symptoms such as colic and constipation [14:42].
- She tied this to diet: a Swedish public health warning, two days earlier, that children eat more processed food and little fibre [14:42].
- Own cohort: 300 children, over 1,000 samples, over 300,000 health data points, with universities in Sweden, Denmark and Belgium [14:42].
- Company: started three years earlier with a professor co-founder; 2.6 million raised in pre-seed in the past year; "well over 1000 tests since the fall"; a market opportunity she put at 80 billion; publications in microbiome science rose to 20,000 a year in 2024 [14:42].
- Each sample carries answers to over 300 questions on diet, probiotic and formula use; Alba Health complies with GDPR and uses a consent form [51:58].
- On whether testing makes parents of healthy children anxious: she said they have not seen it; results are delivered on a call with a nutrition and health coach, with doctors for children with symptoms [55:45].
- Her differentiator: scientific credibility with universities and doctors plus very simple explanations, both in-house [51:58].
- The moderator described Alba Health as a Danish-Swedish company using machine learning and metagenomics [13:38]. Figures date from February 2025.
