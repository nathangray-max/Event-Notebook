# Biotics Unleashed: How microbiome science and health tech transform consumer choices

## Summary
Ewa Hudson of Lumina Intelligence presented consumer survey data on biotics and health tech. The deck is the short version released for distribution. Her question: how changing health goals, medicines such as GLP-1 drugs and technology shape what consumers buy for their microbiome.

The data come from Lumina's Global Nutrition Research for NutraIngredients, November 2024, with 6,000 respondents. In the five European countries shown, ageing in good health and better sleep are the top two health goals. Sleep, stress and mental health lead for younger adults; longevity enters the top three at 45 to 54 and leads from 55.

Europeans who take biotics do so mainly for the gut microbiome, general wellbeing and immunity. Few take them for sleep or mood, or alongside antibiotics. She set this against two numbers: 84% think they lead a healthy life, yet 70% took antibiotics, GLP-1 drugs, laxatives, metformin or proton-pump inhibitors in the past two years.

Her close was technology. Over half use health or fitness apps, and almost all of those buy a product an app recommends at least once a year. Her message: tech is changing behaviour, so build new habits on it.

## Key takeaways
- Ageing in good health and longevity ranks first or second as a health goal in all five countries; better sleep ranks first in France and the UK; mood and mental health ranks first in Spain (slide 5).
- By age, sleep, stress and mental health are the top three goals from 18 to 44; longevity joins at 45 to 54 and leads at 55 to 64 and 65 plus, with sleep and weight, read from the chart (slide 6).
- Why Europeans take biotic supplements, food and beverages: to improve the gut microbiome 15%, general wellbeing 14%, immunity 14%, digestive issues 10%, energy 8%, sleep 6%; alongside antibiotics only 4% (slide 7).
- 84% believe they lead a very or fairly healthy life, but 70% took antibiotics, GLP-1 drugs, laxatives, metformin or proton-pump inhibitors in the past two years; regular users: antibiotics 7%, GLP-1 receptor agonists 5%, laxatives 8% (slide 8).
- 54% use health or fitness apps or trackers, and 93% of them buy an app-recommended supplement, sports nutrition product or meal plan at least once a year: 26% monthly, 52% quarterly, 22% once a year (slide 9).

## For Vita Actives
- Relevance medium: consumer data on health goals and buying, not on our ingredients. It touches us through fitness, energy and healthy ageing, and through apps as a route to sports nutrition buyers.
- Optimising fitness and performance ranks third in Germany, fourth in France, fifth in Spain, sixth in Italy and tenth in the UK; energy ranks fourth in Spain and the UK (slide 5). My reading: performance is a mid-table goal in Europe; healthy ageing leads, which supports pitching CreaWin and IsoClr: Pea to healthy-ageing brands within each ingredient's own evidence.
- 93% of app users buy an app-recommended supplement, sports nutrition product or meal plan at least once a year (slide 9). My reading: fitness apps are a recommendation channel our sports nutrition customers could use; a lead for their marketing, not ours.

## Detail
- The deck is the "for distribution" short version; slide 2 says she examined how changing health goals and medication use, "particularly GLP-1 agonists", influence microbiome health and buying, and offers the full version for download (slide 2). The link is not in the PDF.
- Survey base: Global Nutrition Research (Nov 2024), 29 questions, 6,000 respondents, 9 countries (China, France, Germany, Italy, Japan, South Korea, Spain, the UK, the USA), in four parts: general wellness, health tech, biotics, sports and active (slide 4). The charts on slides 5 to 9 are sourced to 5 European countries (France, Italy, Germany, Spain, UK), still marked n=6,000 (slide 5). Which base each chart uses is to confirm.
- Health goal ranks, France, Germany, Italy, Spain, UK: ageing in good health and longevity 2, 1, 1, 2, 2; better sleep 1, 2, 2, 3, 1; mood and mental health 5, 5, 5, 1, 6; managing stress 3, 7, 3, 7, 5; healthier weight 6, 4, 8, 5, 3; fitness and performance 4, 3, 6, 5, 10; energy 7, 8, 7, 4, 4; immunity 8, 6, 4, 10, 7; digestive health 9, 10, 9, 8, 8; skin 10, 9, 10, 9, 9. Hunger and insulin peaks, hormonal health and fertility, and menopause rank 11 to 13 everywhere (slide 5).
- The bottom three goals at every age are fertility and hormonal health, hunger and menopause, in shifting order, read from the chart (slide 6).
- Other reasons for taking biotics: heart health 5%; mood, anxiety, depression and stress 5%; reducing side effects of drugs other than antibiotics 5%; skin 5%; weight loss or preventing obesity 4%; women's health 3%; men's health 2%; first 1,000 days 1% (slide 7).
- Healthy life: very healthy 15%, fairly healthy 73%, not very healthy 11% (slide 8). The slide's headline adds "5% of regulars and 8% of irregular users in Europe"; it does not say of what (slide 8).
- App users by purpose, read from the chart: diets 19%, fertility 10%, fitness 33%, mind and mood about 14%, overall health about 18%, menopause and pregnancy small (slide 9).
- Slide 3 describes Lumina Intelligence as consumer research for ingredient suppliers, brand owners and retailers (slide 3). Lumina is part of the William Reed group, which runs NutraIngredients (lumina-new-longevity-consumer).
- Figures are from a November 2024 survey, presented in February 2025. No slide is marked Confidential.
- Cross-reference with her November 2026 talk (lumina-new-longevity-consumer): there, 22% named probiotics among the categories most helpful for healthspan, and 6.1% of UK adults currently used GLP-1 drugs. Here, 5% of Europeans are regular GLP-1 users (slide 8). Different surveys, dates and questions; both are given.
