--- page 21 ---
TLDR: Some aging-associated
processes in the body may be
protective (e.g., decline in
Bacteroides).
Maybe we shouldn’t give FMTs
from younger to older individuals?
Future work should focus on
improved markers of healthy aging
(e.g., frailty metrics) and functional
outputs from the microbiota.
Targeted interventions, based on
mechanistic knowledge, are
probably better (e.g., probiotics,
prebiotics, postbiotics, drugs, and
diet).
