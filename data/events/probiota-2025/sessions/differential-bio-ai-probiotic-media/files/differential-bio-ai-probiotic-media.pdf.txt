Key factors to improve result transferability of MTP fermentation
Downscale to MTP
Scale down effect
Downscaling to MTP has minimal influence at the single-
cell level. The primary impacts arise from the high surface-
to-volume ratio.
Anaerobic environment
MTP provides a natural environment for self-initiated semi-
anaerobic fermentations. Factors such as shaking and
covering significantly impact fermentation attributes.
Data quality control
Individual wells are not equally positioned relative to one
another on the MTP. General setups and configurations
can be optimized to improve data quality

© Differential Bio ‹nr.›
Scaleminiaturization: little impact on the individual cell physiology
Downscale to MTP
Observations
● MTP fermentation resulted in a higher
intracellular pH, signaling better cell vitality
compared to that in a shaking flask.
● Intracellular pH were similar in MTPs at
different scale and shaking conditions.
Findings
● An MTP well provides sufficient volume space for
single-cell microorganisms such as bacteria and
yeast.
● Downscaling fermentation to the MTP has minimal
physiological impact at the individual cell level.

--- page 15 ---
© Differential Bio ‹nr.›
Scaleminiaturization: higher static OTR led to early cell proliferation
Downscale to MTP
Observations
● Smaller scale has significantly earlier and faster
fermentation
● Qualitative behavior is similar between MTP
and shaking flask
Findings
● High surface to volume ratio caused higher air-
liquid exchange surface, hence higher static
OTR at early stage
● High static OTR drives earlier and higher growth
[1] Hegab, H. M., ElMekawy, A., & Stakenborg, T. (2013). Review of microfluidic microbioreactor technology for high-throughput submerged microbiological cultivation. Biomicrofluidics, 7(2).

--- page 16 ---
© Differential Bio ‹nr.›
Scaleminiaturization: shaking efficiency increased with cell numbers
Downscale to MTP
Observations
Shaking has minimal impact during the early stages of
cell growth but results in higher cell numbers during
the later stages of fermentation.
Findings
Surface tension decreases with accumulated cells,
resulting in improved shaking efficiency, which
ultimately leads to higher cell numbers by the end of
fermentation.
[1] Duetz WA (2007) Microtiter plates as mini-bioreactors: miniaturization of fermentation methods. Trends Microbiol 15:469–475

--- page 17 ---
© Differential Bio ‹nr.›
Create anaerobicity: impact of headspace air and MTP incubation
Downscale to MTP
Observations
Saturating the polystyrene MTP material with nitrogen
significantly reduces and slows cell growth. Similarly,
replacing headspace air with nitrogen also slows cell
growth.
Findings
Fermentative growth is impacted by air in the MTP
material and headspace. However, anaerobicity can
be achieved by preventing air permeability through
'placing in anaerobic’ or applying oxidizers in the
microenvironment.

© Differential Bio ‹nr.›
Data quality: MTP “edge effect” and cover affect results reproducibility
Downscale to MTP
Observations
● Peripheral wells in MTP are more prone to evaporation,
temperature drift, and air diffusion, which can mislead
cellular assays.
● However, sealing the MTP with an airtight seal resulted
in less than 5% CV for both OD600 and pH
measurements.
Findings
● Individual wells are not equal conditions, which needs
special configurations for sensitive cellular assays
● Each MTP well in a plate is qualified as a independent
fermentation vessel after careful configuration of cover
and relative humidity.
[1] Mansoury, M., Hamed, M., Karmustaji, R., Al Hannan, F., & Safrany, S. T. (2021). The edge effect: A global problem. The t rouble with culturing cells in 96-well plates. Biochemistry and biophysics reports, 26, 100987.
[2] Sieben, M., Giese, H., Grosch, J. H., Kauffmann, K., & Büchs, J. (2016). Permeability of currently available microtiter p late sealing tapes fail to fulfil the requirements for aerobic microbial cultivation. Biotechnology journal, 11(12), 1525 -1538.
