Women’s Health | optimizing brain health
•Women are at a higher risk of developing cognitive conditions such as
Alzheimer's disease and dementia compared to men
•Influenced by hormonal fluctuations, genetic factors, differences in brain
structure and function
•Understanding sex-specific risks is crucial for developing targeted
prevention and treatment strategies for women's brain health
•Need to understand role of the Microbiome in sex-specific
healthy cognition
•Cognitive and mood changes -before, during and after menopausal
transition
•By 2030 -1.2 billion menopausal and post-menopausal women
it is necessary to develop innovative strategies to improve cognitive
health for what can be up to 1/3 of a woman’s life

Athletic mental health
•Sports performance anxiety -incredibly common -about 35% of elite athletes have mental health concerns
•Athletes -increased risk of developing anxiety and depression due to high physical and mental demands
•Factors such as performance pressure, injuries, overtraining, and the need to maintain peak condition can
contribute to psychological distress
• Female athletes tend to report sports anxiety more frequently
•Additionally -transition out of competitive sports and societal expectations can further impact their mental well-
being

microbiome mediated interventions to
reduce Sports Anxiety and improve
performance
